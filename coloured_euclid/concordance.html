Byrne–Simson–Heath–G-Index Concordance¶

Cross-Repository Alignment Across geometor/byrne, geometor/euclid, geometor/elements & geometor/codes

Because Oliver Byrne adopted Robert Simson's 1756 Glasgow numbering, several Book I definitions and axioms carry numbers distinct from the critical Heiberg/Heath text (1908, geometor/euclid) and the geometor/elements G-Index (see Preliminaries & Foundations).
