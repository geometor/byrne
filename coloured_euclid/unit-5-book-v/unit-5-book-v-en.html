Byrne’s Radical Leap in Book V: Abstract Colored Magnitude Tokens (p. 154)¶

In Book V, Eudoxus and Euclid generalize ratio and proportion from spatial lines to arbitrary continuous magnitudes of the same kind—lengths, planar areas, polyhedral volumes, angles, or time intervals. Recognizing that line diagrams obscure this universality, Oliver Byrne abandons spatial figures altogether across pages 153–212 and introduces four typed colored magnitude symbols:

  • First magnitude: (:byrne-mag:`red circle`)

  • Second magnitude: (:byrne-mag:`yellow square`)

  • Third magnitude: (:byrne-mag:`blue diamond`)

  • Fourth magnitude: (:byrne-mag:`black shield`)
