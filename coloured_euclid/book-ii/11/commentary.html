Commentary on Proposition II.11: Exact Q(phi) Area Cancellation & Gateway to the Pentagon¶

Pickering Folio 0100 (p. 66) & Symbolic Golden Ratio Identity (II.11 · G.154)

Symbolic Verification in $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$¶

In geometor.byrne.constructions.build_ii11(), setting $|AB| = 1$ yields exact coordinates in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}) = \mathbb{Q}(\phi)$:

\[|EB| = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}, \qquad |AH| = |AF| = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2} = \phi^{-1}, \qquad |HB| = 1 - \phi^{-1} = \frac{3 - \sqrt{5}}{2} = \phi^{-2}.\]

Both the blue golden square and the yellow complement rectangle simplify in SymPy to the exact irrational area $\frac{3 - \sqrt{5}}{2} = 2 - \phi$, with difference identically 0. Notice how Byrne styles the divided line as red solid () concatenated with red dotted () so whole and parts remain visually linked yet algebraically distinct!
