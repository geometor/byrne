Commentary on Proposition II.5: Gnomon Surgery & Completing the Square¶

Pickering Folio 0092 (p. 58) & Quadratic Area Conservation (II.5 · G.148)

Algebraic Identity Behind II.5¶

Setting half-length $a = CB$ and offset $x = CD$, the unequal parts are $AD = a + x$ and $DB = a - x$, so Proposition II.5 expresses the universal identity:

\[(a + x)(a - x) + x^2 = a^2.\]

In geometor.byrne.constructions.build_ii5(), $A=(0,0), C=(4,0), D=(6,0), B=(8,0)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ give $AD \cdot DB = 6 \times 2 = 12$ and $CD^2 = 2^2 = 4$ (), summing identically to $CB^2 = 4^2 = 16$. Together with its twin II.6 (external division, used in II.11 and III.36), II.5 forms the geometric engine of Renaissance Cossic quadratic solution (see geometor/pacioli, Summa 1494).
