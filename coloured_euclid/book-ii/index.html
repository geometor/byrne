Book II — Geometric Algebra & The Golden Section¶

Coloured Rectangles, Gnomons & Extreme and Mean Ratio (Pickering pp. 51–70, Leaves 85–104)

Book II translates quadratic algebraic identities into direct visual area decompositions—partitioning squares, contained rectangles (×), and gnomons across the binomial square (II.4), difference of squares (II.5), and the Golden Section (II.11).
