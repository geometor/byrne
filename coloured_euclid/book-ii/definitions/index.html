Book II Definitions (I–II)¶

Contained Rectangles & The Gnomon (Pickering pp. 51–52, Leaves 85–86)

  • Definition I (p. 51): Every right-angled parallelogram () is said to be contained (×) by any two of the straight lines (, ) which contain one of the right angles.

f. 0086 · p. 52

  • Definition II (p. 52): In every parallelogram, any of the parallelograms about a diameter, together with the two complements, is called a gnomon.
