The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid¶

In Which Coloured Diagrams and Symbols Are Used Instead of Letters for the Greater Ease of Learners — Oliver Byrne (1847)

Author: Oliver Byrne
First Edition (London, 1847): Published by William Pickering, printed by Charles Whittingham at the Chiswick Press, 1847 (Internet Archive firstsixbooksofe00eucl).
Euclidean Base Text: Robert Simson's 1756 Glasgow Edition (35 Definitions, 3 Postulates, 12 Axioms).
Digital Critical Codex: Dedicated per-proposition pages across Books I–VI with synchronized Proposition · Commentary tracks, Pickering 1847 folio links, interactive inline SVG color tokens, and exact geometor.model plates.

“Segnius irritant animos demissa per aurem, Quam quæ sunt oculis subjecta fidelibus, et quæ Ipse sibi tradit spectator.”

“What finds entrance through the ear strikes the mind less vividly than what is brought before the trusty eyes of the spectator himself.”

—Horace, Ars Poetica 180–182 (quoted by Oliver Byrne, Introduction, p. viii)
