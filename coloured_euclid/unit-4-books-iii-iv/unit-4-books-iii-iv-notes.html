Solving the Five-Edge Pentagon Paradox on a Four-Ink Press¶

Book IV, Proposition 11 (Pickering p. 137, Leaf 171) poses a crisp combinatorial test for Oliver Byrne: a regular pentagon has five distinct perimeter chords, yet the Chiswick Press palette has only four inks (red, blue, yellow, black), and the inscribed Golden Triangle already occupies solid yellow ()!
