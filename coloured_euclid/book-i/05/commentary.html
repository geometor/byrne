Why I.5 Earned the Name Pons Asinorum—and How Byrne Tames It¶

In classical alphabetic editions, Proposition I.5 requires 329 words and 62 three-letter vertex-pointer dereferences because four overlapping triangles (AFC, AGB, BFC, CGB) share vertices A, B, C, F, G. On Pickering p. 5 (Leaf 39), Byrne assigns bilateral mirror twins (, , , , ), compressing the proof to 32 words (90.3% compression) and zero pointer lookups.
