Archival & Symbolic Commentary on Book I

Archival Note: The 1847 Title-Page Transposition of I.47¶

Direct multimodal inspection of the 1847 Pickering volume reveals a telling bibliographic detail between Leaf 7 (Title Page) and Leaves 82–83 (pp. 48–49):

  • On pp. 48–49 (the proof of I.47), the narrow left hypotenuse rectangle under the short leg is printed in ochre yellow (, equal to the black leg square ) and the wide right hypotenuse rectangle under the long leg is printed in Prussian blue (, equal to the red leg square ).

  • On the 1847 Title Page emblem (Leaf 7), Whittingham's pressman transposed the yellow and blue woodblocks on the bottom square, coloring the narrow left strip blue and the wide right rectangle yellow. Our symbolic i47.svg plate faithfully follows the mathematically canonical proof plate on pp. 48–49.

Chromatic Register Allocation Across Book I¶

Across Book I’s seven showcase plates, Byrne demonstrates every core idiom of his visual language:

  • Direct radius sharing in I.1, where two intersecting colored circles turn three segment equalities into an immediate visual transitivity chain.

  • Congruence-twin pairing in I.4 and I.5, assigning identical ink colors to symmetric segments and angles so SAS superposition becomes self-evident.
