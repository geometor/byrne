Pickering p. 15: Pure Visual Equations¶

Pickering p. 15 (Leaf 49) is one of the purest demonstrations in Byrne’s Elements. Because four rays meeting at a point partition the full circle into exactly four sectors, Chiswick Press’s four inks (, , , ) saturate the palette 1-to-1 without a single auxiliary stroke modifier. Subtracting the shared sector from two equations turns I.15 into a three-line visual cancellation.

Symbolic Verification over $\mathbb{Q}$¶

In geometor.byrne.constructions.build_i15(), intersecting rational lines $AB$ and $CD$ at $E = (0, 0)$ yields exact equality of opposite spreads $s(EB, EC) = s(EA, ED)$ and $s(EC, EA) = s(ED, EB)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$—reflecting the projective fact that in Rational Trigonometry two lines define a single well-defined spread independent of ray orientation.
