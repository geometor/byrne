Commentary on Proposition I.46: Constructing the Orthogonal Unit Before the Pythagorean Climax¶

Pickering Folio 0081 Verification & Parallel Metric Foundations (I.46 · G.139)

Pickering p. 47: Four Sides, Four Quadrants, Four Inks¶

On Pickering p. 47 (Leaf 81), Byrne assigns each side of the constructed square a distinct ink (, , , ) and prints four quarter-disk right-angle sectors in permuted colors (, , , ) so every edge and every vertex angle remains uniquely addressable without a single letter. Notice that Byrne cites Simson’s Definition XXVII (where Simson defines a square purely by four equal sides and four right angles, avoiding Euclid’s redundant rhombic/rectangular clauses).

Exact Symbolic Model in geometor.byrne¶

In geometor.byrne.constructions.build_i46(), $A=(0,0), B=(3,0), C=(0,3), D=(3,3)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ verify equal side quadrances $Q = 9$ and unit orthogonal spreads $s(AB, AC) = s(CA, CD) = 1$, serving immediately as the subroutine invoked three times in Proposition I.47.
