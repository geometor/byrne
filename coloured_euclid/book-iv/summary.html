Book IV Summary: From the Incircle to the Regular Pentagon¶

How Books I, II & III Converge in Book IV, Propositions 4, 10 & 11

  1. The Incenter & Equal Perpendicular Radii (:ref:`IV.4 <byrne-iv-4>`): Bisecting two interior angles (I.9) locates the incenter (Kimberling X(1) in geometor/etc), whose three dotted perpendiculars (I.12) are proved equal by AAS congruence (I.26) so the circle touches all three sides (III.16).

  2. The Pentagonal Crown (:ref:`IV.10 <byrne-iv-10>` → :ref:`IV.11 <byrne-iv-11>`): Cutting a radius in golden ratio via II.11 and establishing tangent contact via III.37 constructs the $36^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ$ Golden Triangle (IV.10), whose bisected base angles immediately inscribe the regular pentagon (IV.11).
