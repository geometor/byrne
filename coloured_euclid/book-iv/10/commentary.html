Commentary on Proposition IV.10: Golden Ratio Meets Tangent-Chord Geometry in Q(phi, sqrt(10 - 2*sqrt(5)))¶

Pickering Folios 0169–0170 (pp. 135–136) & Cyclotomic Field Verification (IV.10 · G.223)

Architectural Keystone of Books I–IV¶

Pickering pp. 135–136 (Leaves 169–170) unite four books of the Elements in a single two-circle plate ( and ):

  1. Golden area division (II.11) supplies $|AB| \cdot |CB| = |AC|^2$;

  2. Setting $|BD| = |AC|$ converts that area equality into tangent-secant power $|AB| \cdot |CB| = |BD|^2$, proving $BD$ tangent to circumcircle $(ACD)$ (III.37);

  3. Tangent-chord equality (III.32) transfers the $36^\circ$ apex angle into $\triangle BCD$, revealing three equal $\phi^{-1}$ segments ($AC = CD = BD$) and base angles $72^\circ = 2 \times 36^\circ$.

Exact Symbolic Field in geometor.byrne¶

In geometor.byrne.constructions.build_iv10(), unit legs $|AB| = |AD| = 1$ and base $|BD| = \phi^{-1} = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$ lie in $\mathbb{Q}(\phi, \sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}})$, with apex spread $s_{36^\circ} = \frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{8}$ and base spread $s_{72^\circ} = \frac{5 + \sqrt{5}}{8} = 4 s_{36^\circ}(1 - s_{36^\circ})$.
