Book IV — Inscribed Circles, The Golden Triangle & Regular Pentagon¶

Incircles, Cyclotomic Symmetry & Chromatic Register Mastery (Pickering pp. 125–151, Leaves 159–185)

Book IV unites the triangle congruence of Book I, the golden-section area algebra of Book II (II.11), and the tangent-chord circle theorems of Book III (III.36–III.37) to construct inscribed circles (IV.4), the $36^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ$ Golden Triangle (IV.10), and the regular pentagon (IV.11).

Propositions in Book IV¶

Proposition IV.10 — Isosceles Golden Triangle

To construct an isosceles triangle, in which each of the angles at the base shall be double of the vertical angle.
