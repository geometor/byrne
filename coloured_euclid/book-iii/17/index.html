Proposition III.17 — Drawing a Tangent from an Exterior Point¶

Book III, Proposition XVII. Problem (Pickering pp. 98–99)

To draw a tangent to a given circle from a given point, either in or outside of its circumference.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record¶

  • Archival Folio Witness: Leaf 132 (pp. 98–99)

  • Chiswick Press Visual Design: Concentric red inner and yellow outer circles; dotted black + solid black horizontal radius, vertical blue tangent to inner circle, red+dotted-red radius to outer circle, dotted blue line from outer point, and blue and yellow angle sectors.

  • Cited Deductive Dependencies: [III.16], [I.4]
