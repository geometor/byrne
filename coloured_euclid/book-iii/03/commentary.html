Commentary on Proposition III.3: Diameter Bisects a Chord Perpendicularly¶

Pickering Folio 0110 (p. 76) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure¶

In Proposition III.3, Oliver Byrne renders Euclid's theorem from Robert Simson's text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue circle with vertical solid+dotted black diameter perpendicular to horizontal chord (solid red left, dotted red right), yellow and blue radii, and blue, red, black, and yellow angle sectors.

Logical Citations in Print: [I.8], [I.def.7], [I.5], [I.26].
