Book V Summary: Eudoxian Proportion as Typed Colored Algebra¶

Why Book V Is Byrne’s Most Modern Algebraic Innovation

  1. Redundant Shape + Color Dual Coding: Byrne pairs distinct shapes (circle, square, diamond, shield, pentagon, triangle) with his four colors (red, yellow, blue, black), giving every term of a four- or six-magnitude proportion a unique silhouette even without color cues.

  2. Elimination of False Metric Intuition: Classical editions draw line segments of fixed lengths for Book V propositions, tempting learners to assume commensurability. Byrne’s abstract tokens faithfully preserve Eudoxus’s relational axioms across rational and irrational magnitudes ($\sqrt{2}$, $\phi$) alike.

  3. Verbal Compression Record (92.9% Reduction): Across V.11, V.16, V.18, and V.22, writing integer multipliers ($M \cdot$ , $m \cdot$ ) directly eliminates the explosion of auxiliary Greek/Latin letters ($E, F, G, H, K, L$) needed for equimultiples in classical prose.
