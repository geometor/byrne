Commentary on Proposition V.18: Componendo, Existence of the Fourth Proportional & Golden Ratio Recurrence¶

Pickering Folio 0219 (p. 185) & Axiom of Completeness Note (V.18 · G.278)

Historical & Logical Commentary on V.18¶

Pickering p. 185 (Leaf 219) preserves Euclid’s indirect reductio proof of componendo from dividendo (V.17), representing the hypothetical distinct fourth proportional by a solid black disk . As Simson and Heath noted in their critical apparatus (compare geometor/euclid), Euclid’s indirect step tacitly assumes the existence of a fourth proportional—whereas direct equimultiple manipulation via V.def.5 proves V.18 constructively without any completeness postulate.

In Golden Ratio algebra, componendo transforms $1 : \phi^{-1} \mathrel{∷} \phi^{-1} : \phi^{-2}$ immediately into $\phi : 1 \mathrel{∷} 1 : \phi^{-1}$ ($\phi = 1 + \phi^{-1}$).
