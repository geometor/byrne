Book VI — Similar Figures & Extreme and Mean Ratio¶

Proportional Geometry & Application of Areas (Pickering pp. 213–268, Leaves 247–302)

Book VI reunites the abstract Eudoxian proportion calculus of Book V with planar figures—developing similarity, parallel proportion, mean proportionals, and the proportional division of a line into extreme and mean ratio (VI.30).

Book VI Key Definitions & Deductive Arc (pp. 213–259)¶

  • Definition I. Similar rectilineal figures are those which have their several angles equal, each to each, and the sides about the equal angles proportionals (∷).

  • Definition III. A straight line is said to be cut in extreme and mean ratio (Golden Section) when the whole line is to the greater segment as the greater segment is to the less.

  • Props. VI.1–VI.13 (``G.286``–``G.298``): Proportional triangles, parallel intercept theorem, angle bisector ratio, and semicircular geometric mean $\sqrt{ab}$.

  • Prop. VI.31 (p. 259 · ``G.329``): Generalized Pythagorean Theorem extending I.47 to any three similar and similarly described rectilineal figures.
