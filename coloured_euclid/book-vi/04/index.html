Proposition VI.4 — Equiangular Triangles Have Proportional Sides¶

Book VI, Proposition IV. Theorem (Pickering pp. 218–219)

IN equiangular triangles ((black-and-colored-outline triangle) and (dotted-outline triangle)) the sides about the equal angles are proportional, and the sides which are opposite to the equal angles are homologous.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record¶

  • Archival Folio Witness: Leaf 252 (pp. 218–219)

  • Chiswick Press Visual Design: Two equiangular triangles (solid blue/red/black left, dotted blue/red/black right) placed on a collinear base, with dotted yellow and solid yellow upper extensions meeting at the apex to form a parallelogram; equal angles are marked with yellow, blue, red, and black sectors and black and red arcs.
