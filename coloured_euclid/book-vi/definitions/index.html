Book VI Definitions (I–IV)¶

Similarity, Reciprocity, Extreme & Mean Ratio and Altitude (Pickering p. [211], Leaf 245)

  • Definition I: Similar rectilineal figures are those which have their several angles equal, each to each, and the sides about the equal angles proportionals (∷).

  • Definition II: Two figures are reciprocally proportional when each has antecedent and consequent sides reversing between the two figures.

  • Definition III: A straight line is said to be cut in extreme and mean ratio (Golden Section) when the whole line is to the greater segment as the greater segment is to the less.

  • Definition IV: The altitude of any figure is the straight line drawn from its vertex perpendicular (⊥) to the base.

See Commentary.
