Proposition IV.16 — Regular Quindecagon Inscribed in a Circle
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Book IV, Proposition XVI. Problem (*Pickering p. 144*)

To inscribe an equilateral and equiangular quindecagon in a given circle.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 178 (p. 144)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** A blue circle containing an inscribed equilateral triangle (yellow left side, black other two sides) and an inscribed regular pentagon (red and blue upper-left sides, black other sides), with a short black dotted chord spanning the 1/15th arc difference at the lower left.
