Commentary on Proposition IV.16: Regular Quindecagon Inscribed in a Circle
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0178 (p. 144) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.16 <byrne-iv-16>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A blue circle containing an inscribed equilateral triangle (yellow left side, black other two sides) and an inscribed regular pentagon (red and blue upper-left sides, black other sides), with a short black dotted chord spanning the 1/15th arc difference at the lower left.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`IV.1`.
