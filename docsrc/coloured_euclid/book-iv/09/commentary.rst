Commentary on Proposition IV.9: Circumscribing a Circle About a Square
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0168 (p. 134) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.9 <byrne-iv-9>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A black circle circumscribing a square divided diagonally into red (upper-right) and yellow (lower-left) triangles, with blue/black dotted-and-solid diagonals and yellow, black, red, and blue corner angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.8`, :just:`I.6`.
