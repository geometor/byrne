Commentary on Proposition IV.15: Regular Hexagon Inscribed in a Circle
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0177 (p. 143) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.15 <byrne-iv-15>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A yellow given circle intersected by a lower red circle passing through its center; three diameters (black vertical, blue, yellow) define an inscribed hexagon (solid black upper sides, dotted black, dotted red, dotted blue lower/right sides) with red, blue, and black central angle sectors.
