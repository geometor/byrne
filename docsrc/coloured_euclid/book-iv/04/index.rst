Proposition IV.4 — Incircle of a Triangle
=========================================

.. rubric:: Book IV, Proposition IV. Problem (*Pickering p. 129* · ``G.217``)

*In a given triangle* (:byrne-tri:`yellow/red/black <iv4:< A B C >>`) *to inscribe a circle.*

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iv4.svg
   :alt: Book IV Proposition 4 — Incircle of a triangle constructed from two blue angle bisectors and three dotted perpendicular inradii.
   :caption: **Book IV, Prop. IV (p. 129 · G.217).** Inscribing :byrne-circle:`black <iv4:( I E )>` inside a given triangle (:byrne-line:`yellow solid <iv4:/ A B />`, :byrne-line:`red solid <iv4:/ A C />`, :byrne-line:`black solid <iv4:/ B C />`).

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Construction.** Bisect the two base angles (:just:`I.9`) by :byrne-line:`blue dotted <iv4:/ B I />` and :byrne-line:`blue solid <iv4:/ C I />`; from the point where these lines meet draw :byrne-line:`black dotted <iv4:/ I E />`, :byrne-line:`yellow dotted <iv4:/ I F />`, and :byrne-line:`red dotted <iv4:/ I G />` respectively perpendicular (:byrne-op:`perp`) to :byrne-line:`black solid <iv4:/ B C />`, :byrne-line:`yellow solid <iv4:/ A B />`, and :byrne-line:`red solid <iv4:/ A C />` (:just:`I.12`).

   **Demonstration (:just:`I.26`, :just:`C.N.1`, :just:`III.16`).**

   In the two right triangles sharing hypotenuse :byrne-line:`blue dotted <iv4:/ B I />`, the half-angles at the vertex are equal (const.) and the right angles at the feet are equal, with :byrne-line:`blue dotted <iv4:/ B I />` common;

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-line:`yellow dotted <iv4:/ I F />` = :byrne-line:`black dotted <iv4:/ I E />` (:just:`I.26`).

   In like manner, along bisector :byrne-line:`blue solid <iv4:/ C I />`, :byrne-line:`red dotted <iv4:/ I G />` = :byrne-line:`black dotted <iv4:/ I E />`,

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-line:`black dotted <iv4:/ I E />` = :byrne-line:`yellow dotted <iv4:/ I F />` = :byrne-line:`red dotted <iv4:/ I G />` (:just:`C.N.1`).

   Hence with any one of these equal dotted lines as radius describe :byrne-circle:`black <iv4:( I E )>`: it passes through the extremities of the other two, and the three sides (:byrne-line:`black solid <iv4:/ B C />`, :byrne-line:`yellow solid <iv4:/ A B />`, :byrne-line:`red solid <iv4:/ A C />`), being perpendicular to the three radii at their extremities, touch :byrne-circle:`black <iv4:( I E )>` (:just:`III.16`). **Q.E.F.**
