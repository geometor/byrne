Book IV — Inscribed Circles, The Golden Triangle & Regular Pentagon
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Incircles, Cyclotomic Symmetry & Chromatic Register Mastery (*Pickering pp. 123–144, Leaves 157–178*)

.. folio:: 0157
   :page: 123

Book IV unites the triangle congruence of Book I, the golden-section area algebra of Book II (:ref:`II.11 <byrne-ii-11>`), and the tangent-chord circle theorems of Book III (:ref:`III.36 <byrne-iii-36>`–``III.37``) to construct inscribed circles (:ref:`IV.4 <byrne-iv-4>`), the $36^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ$ Golden Triangle (:ref:`IV.10 <byrne-iv-10>`), and the regular pentagon (:ref:`IV.11 <byrne-iv-11>`).
