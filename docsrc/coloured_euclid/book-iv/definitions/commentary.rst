Commentary on Book IV Definitions
=================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Leaves 157–158 (pp. 123–124)

Book IV's seven symmetric definitions formalize mutual inscription and circumscription between polygons and circles, paving the way for the incircle (:ref:`IV.4 <byrne-iv-4>`), circumcircle (``IV.5``), regular pentagon (:ref:`IV.11 <byrne-iv-11>`), and quindecagon (``IV.16``).
