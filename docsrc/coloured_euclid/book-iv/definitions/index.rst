Book IV Definitions (I–VII)
===========================

.. rubric:: Inscribed & Circumscribed Rectilineal Figures and Circles (*Pickering pp. 123–124, Leaves 157–158*)

.. picture:: 0157.g1.png
   :alt: Book IV Definition inscribed triangle inside blue circle (Leaf 157, p. 123).
   :caption: **Book IV Definitions Archival Plate (Leaf 157, p. 123).** Rectilineal figure inscribed in a circle.

.. picture:: 0157.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram of Book IV inscribed rectilineal figure.
   :caption: **Calibrated Vector Diagram (0157.g1).** Theme-adaptive vector plate of an inscribed triangle inside a circle.

* **Definitions I–II (p. 123):** A rectilineal figure is *inscribed in* another when each angular point of the inscribed figure lies on a side of the other; and conversely *circumscribed about* it.

.. folio:: 0158
   :page: 124

* **Definitions III–VII (p. 124):** A rectilineal figure is *inscribed in* a circle when each angular point is on the circumference; a circle is *inscribed in* or *circumscribed about* a rectilineal figure; and a straight line is *fitted into* (*applied in*) a circle when its extremities lie on the circumference.
