Commentary on Proposition IV.8: Inscribing a Circle in a Square
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0167 (p. 133) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.8 <byrne-iv-8>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A square divided into four quadrants (top-left white, top-right black, bottom-left red, bottom-right blue) with dashed/solid blue, red, black, and yellow midlines and outer edges, enclosing an inscribed yellow circle.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.31`, *hyp*, :just:`I.34`, :just:`III.16`.
