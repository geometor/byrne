Commentary on Proposition IV.7: Circumscribing a Square About a Circle
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0166 (p. 132) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.7 <byrne-iv-7>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A blue circle with perpendicular dotted blue and dotted red diameters, circumscribed by a square with black top, yellow right, blue bottom, and red left sides, with a black central quadrant, yellow tangent right angle, and four red corner angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.18`, *const*, :just:`I.34`.
