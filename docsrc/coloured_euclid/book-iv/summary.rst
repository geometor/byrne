Book IV Summary: From the Incircle to the Regular Pentagon
==========================================================

.. rubric:: How Books I, II & III Converge in Book IV, Propositions 4, 10 & 11

.. folio:: 0157
   :page: 123

1. **The Incenter & Equal Perpendicular Radii (:ref:`IV.4 <byrne-iv-4>`)**:
   Bisecting two interior angles (``I.9``) locates the incenter (Kimberling ``X(1)`` in ``geometor/etc``), whose three dotted perpendiculars (``I.12``) are proved equal by ``AAS`` congruence (``I.26``) so the circle touches all three sides (``III.16``).
2. **The Pentagonal Crown (:ref:`IV.10 <byrne-iv-10>` → :ref:`IV.11 <byrne-iv-11>`)**:
   Cutting a radius in golden ratio via :ref:`II.11 <byrne-ii-11>` and establishing tangent contact via ``III.37`` constructs the :math:`36^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ` Golden Triangle (:ref:`IV.10 <byrne-iv-10>`), whose bisected base angles immediately inscribe the regular pentagon (:ref:`IV.11 <byrne-iv-11>`).
