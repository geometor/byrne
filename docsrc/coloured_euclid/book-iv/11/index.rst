Proposition IV.11 — Regular Pentagon Inscribed in a Circle
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Book IV, Proposition XI. Problem (*Pickering p. 137* · ``G.224``)

*In a given circle* (:byrne-circle:`blue <iv11:( O A )>`) *to inscribe an equilateral and equiangular pentagon.*

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iv11.svg
   :alt: Book IV Proposition 11 — Regular pentagon inscribed in a blue circle around a solid yellow Golden Triangle.
   :caption: **Book IV, Prop. XI (p. 137 · G.224).** Regular pentagon inscribed in :byrne-circle:`blue <iv11:( O A )>` by bisecting the base angles of inscribed Golden Triangle :byrne-tri:`yellow filled <iv11:< A C D >>`.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Construction.** Construct an isosceles Golden Triangle (:just:`IV.10`) and inscribe in :byrne-circle:`blue <iv11:( O A )>` a triangle :byrne-tri:`yellow filled <iv11:< A C D >>` equiangular to it (:just:`IV.2`). Bisect both base angles (:just:`I.9`) by star diagonals :byrne-line:`black solid <iv11:/ C E /; / D B />` meeting the circumference, and draw perimeter chords :byrne-line:`red solid <iv11:/ A B />`, :byrne-line:`blue solid <iv11:/ E A />`, :byrne-line:`yellow solid <iv11:/ D E />`, and :byrne-line:`red dotted <iv11:/ B C />`.

   **Demonstration (:just:`III.26`, :just:`III.29`, :just:`III.27`).**

   Because each of the five $36^\circ$ inscribed angles (:byrne-angle:`black <iv11:< C A D )>`, :byrne-angle:`blue <iv11:< E C A )>`, :byrne-angle:`black <iv11:< D C E )>`, :byrne-angle:`red <iv11:< B D C )>`, and the remaining bisected half-angle) are equal, the five arcs upon which they stand are equal (:just:`III.26`),

   :byrne-op:`therefore` the five subtending chords :byrne-line:`black solid <iv11:/ C D />`, :byrne-line:`red solid <iv11:/ A B />`, :byrne-line:`blue solid <iv11:/ E A />`, :byrne-line:`yellow solid <iv11:/ D E />`, and :byrne-line:`red dotted <iv11:/ B C />` are equal (:just:`III.29`);

   and the pentagon is also equiangular, as each interior angle stands upon an equal three-fifths arc (:just:`III.27`). **Q.E.F.**
