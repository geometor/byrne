Commentary on Proposition IV.2: Inscribing a Triangle Equiangular to a Given Triangle
=====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0160 (p. 126) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.2 <byrne-iv-2>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A black circle tangent below to a red horizontal line, with an inscribed triangle (yellow top side, black lateral sides) having yellow, blue, black, and outlined angle sectors, paired to the right with a separate given triangle having blue, yellow, and red angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.17`, :just:`I.23`, *const*, :just:`III.32`, :just:`I.32`.
