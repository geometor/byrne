Proposition IV.1 — Placing a Chord Equal to a Given Line
========================================================

.. rubric:: Book IV, Proposition I. Problem (*Pickering p. 125*)

In a given circle :byrne-circle:`yellow` to place a straight line, equal to a given straight line (:byrne-line:`blue solid`), not greater than the diameter of the circle.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 159 (p. 125)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** A yellow given circle with a horizontal red dotted/solid diameter, intersected by a larger blue circle centered at the left extremity of the diameter, a yellow chord to the upper intersection, and a short blue reference line below.
