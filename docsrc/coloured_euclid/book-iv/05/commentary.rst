Commentary on Proposition IV.5: Circumcircle of a Triangle
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0164 (p. 130) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.5 <byrne-iv-5>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Three black circles stacked vertically on the left illustrating the three cases (center inside, on a side, and outside the triangle), each triangle having blue and red solid/dotted sides, yellow solid/dotted perpendicular bisectors, black solid/dotted radii, and black/red right-angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.10`, :just:`I.11`, *const*, :just:`I.4`.
