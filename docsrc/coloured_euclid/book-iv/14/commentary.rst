Commentary on Proposition IV.14: Circumscribing a Circle About a Regular Pentagon
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0176 (p. 142) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.14 <byrne-iv-14>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A red circle circumscribing a pentagon (blue left, red bottom, black-dotted right, black upper sides) with dotted red, dotted blue, dotted yellow, solid yellow, and solid black lines from the center to the five vertices, plus black, yellow, red, and blue angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.6`, :just:`I.4`.
