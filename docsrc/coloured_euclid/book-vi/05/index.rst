Proposition VI.5 — Triangles with Proportional Sides Are Equiangular
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition V. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 220–221*)

IF two triangles have their sides proportional (:byrne-line:`blue dotted` : :byrne-line:`black dotted` :: :byrne-line:`blue solid` : :byrne-line:`black solid`) and (:byrne-line:`black dotted` : :byrne-line:`red dotted` :: :byrne-line:`black solid` : :byrne-line:`red solid`) they are equiangular, and the equal angles are subtended by the homologous sides.
