Commentary on Proposition VI.8: Altitude in Right Triangle Yields Similar Subtriangles
======================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0260 (p. 226) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.8 <byrne-vi-8>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A right-angled triangle divided by a vertical black perpendicular from the top right angle into a large yellow left triangle and a smaller red right triangle, with black, blue, red, yellow, and black-outlined blue angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** ``B. 1. ax. 11``, :just:`I.32`, :just:`VI.4`, :just:`VI.def.1`.
