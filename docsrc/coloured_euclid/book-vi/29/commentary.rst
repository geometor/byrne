Commentary on Proposition VI.29: Application of Areas Exceeding by a Similar Parallelogram
==========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0291 (p. 257) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.29 <byrne-vi-29>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A red semicircle on a diameter composed of dotted yellow, solid blue, dotted blue, and solid yellow segments, with a dotted red vertical perpendicular, a solid red radius, and a solid black reference line below.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`II.6`, :just:`I.47`.
