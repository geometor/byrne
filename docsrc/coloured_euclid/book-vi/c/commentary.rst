Commentary on Proposition VI.C: Rectangle of Sides Equals Altitude by Circumdiameter
====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0300 (p. 266) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.C <byrne-vi-c>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A triangle (dotted blue, yellow, and dotted red sides) inscribed in a red circle, with a dotted yellow vertical perpendicular to the base, a solid blue diameter from the top vertex, and a solid black closing chord, marked with red, yellow, and blue angle sectors.
