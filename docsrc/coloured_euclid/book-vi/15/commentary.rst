Commentary on Proposition VI.15: Reciprocal Sides of Equal Triangles Sharing an Angle
=====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0268 (pp. 234–235) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.15 <byrne-vi-15>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A red triangle on the left and a yellow triangle on the right meeting at vertically opposite equal angles (blue and red sectors), bridged above by a blue auxiliary triangle with a dotted black top edge.
