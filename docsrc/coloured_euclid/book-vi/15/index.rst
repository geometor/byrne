Proposition VI.15 — Reciprocal Sides of Equal Triangles Sharing an Angle
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XV. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 234–235*)

I. EQUAL triangles, which have one angle in each equal (:byrne-angle:`blue` = :byrne-angle:`red`), have the sides about the equal angles reciprocally proportional (:byrne-line:`blue solid` : :byrne-line:`black solid` :: :byrne-line:`red solid` : :byrne-line:`yellow solid`). II. And two triangles which have an angle of the one equal to an angle of the other, and the sides about the equal angles reciprocally proportional, are equal.
