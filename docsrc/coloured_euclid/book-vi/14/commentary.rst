Commentary on Proposition VI.14: Reciprocal Sides of Equal Equiangular Parallelograms
=====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0266 (pp. 232–233) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.14 <byrne-vi-14>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Three parallelograms meeting at a central vertex: a yellow parallelogram at upper left, a red auxiliary parallelogram at upper right, and a tall blue parallelogram at lower right, with red, black, yellow, and blue edges.
