Proposition VI.24 — Parallelograms About a Diagonal Are Similar to the Whole
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXIV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 250*)

IN any parallelogram ((divided parallelogram with yellow, red, and blue regions)) the parallelograms ((outline parallelogram with red top/left) and (outline parallelogram with blue top/left)) which are about the diagonal are similar to the whole, and to each other.
