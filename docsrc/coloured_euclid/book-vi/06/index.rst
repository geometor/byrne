Proposition VI.6 — SAS Similarity of Triangles
==============================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition VI. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 222–223*)

IF two triangles ((dotted triangle: red, blue, black) and (solid triangle: red, blue, black)) have one angle ((solid red angle)) of the one, equal to one angle ((black-outlined red angle)) of the other, and the sides about the equal angles proportional, the triangles shall be equiangular, and have those angles equal which the homologous sides subtend.
