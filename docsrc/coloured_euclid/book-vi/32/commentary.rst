Commentary on Proposition VI.32: Angle-Linked Proportional Sides Align into Collinearity
========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0294 (p. 260) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.32 <byrne-vi-32>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two triangles meeting at a vertex on a collinear yellow and dotted-yellow baseline: a larger left triangle (blue, red, yellow sides with red, yellow, blue angle sectors) and a smaller right triangle (dotted black, dotted red, dotted yellow sides with yellow and black angle sectors).
