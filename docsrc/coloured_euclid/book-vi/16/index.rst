Proposition VI.16 — Rectangle of Extremes Equals Rectangle of Means
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XVI. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 236–237*)

PART I. IF four straight lines be proportional (:byrne-line:`yellow solid` : :byrne-line:`blue solid` :: :byrne-line:`red dotted` : :byrne-line:`black dotted`), the rectangle (:byrne-line:`yellow solid` × :byrne-line:`black dotted`) contained by the extremes, is equal to the rectangle (:byrne-line:`blue solid` × :byrne-line:`red dotted`) contained by the means. PART II. And if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to the rectangle contained by the means, the four straight lines are proportional.
