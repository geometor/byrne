Commentary on Proposition VI.13: Mean Proportional Between Two Straight Lines
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0265 (p. 231) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.13 <byrne-vi-13>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A red semicircle on a horizontal diameter divided into a blue left segment and a red right segment (with midpoint tick), a vertical solid black mean proportional to the circumference, solid yellow and dotted yellow chords, and a blue right-angle sector at the apex.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.31`, :just:`VI.8`.
