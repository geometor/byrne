Proposition VI.18 — Constructing a Similar Rectilineal Figure on a Given Line
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XVIII. Problem (*Pickering pp. 239–240*)

ON a given straight line (:byrne-line:`black solid`) to construct a rectilinear figure similar to a given one ((pentagon divided into yellow, blue, and red triangles)) and similarly placed.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 273 (pp. 239–240)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Two similar pentagons (larger above, smaller below) each decomposed into yellow, blue, and red triangles by diagonals (dotted red and dotted yellow in the upper, solid black/yellow in the lower), with black, red, and yellow angle arcs. (Note: Byrne's running head misprints 'THEOR.' instead of 'PROB.'; folio 0274 also prints 'B. 6. pr. 22' instead of 'B. 5. pr. 22'.)
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** :just:`I.32`, :just:`VI.4`, :just:`VI.22`, :just:`VI.def.1`
