Commentary on Proposition VI.28: Application of Areas Deficient by a Similar Parallelogram
==========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0289 (pp. 255–256) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.28 <byrne-vi-28>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A horizontal segmented line (dotted red, solid blue, dotted black) with a vertical red perpendicular at the midpoint, a yellow hypotenuse to the left intersection, and a red circular arc cutting the line below.
