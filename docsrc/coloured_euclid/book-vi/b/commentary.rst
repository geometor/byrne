Commentary on Proposition VI.B: Square on Internal Angle Bisector Plus Base Segments Rectangle
==============================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0298 (pp. 264–265) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.B <byrne-vi-b>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A triangle (blue and black sides, dotted-red and solid-red base) inscribed in a yellow circle, with a yellow angle bisector extended in dotted yellow to the circle and joined by a dotted blue chord, with red, blue, yellow, and black angle sectors.
