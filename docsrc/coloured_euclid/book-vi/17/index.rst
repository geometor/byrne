Proposition VI.17 — Rectangle of Extremes Equals Square of Mean Proportional
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XVII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 238*)

PART I. IF three straight lines be proportional (:byrne-line:`red solid` : :byrne-line:`blue solid` :: :byrne-line:`blue solid` : :byrne-line:`black solid`) the rectangle under the extremes is equal to the square of the mean. PART II. And if the rectangle under the extremes be equal to the square of the mean, the three straight lines are proportional.
