Proposition VI.3 — Internal Angle Bisector Divides Opposite Side Proportionally
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition III. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 216–217*)

A RIGHT line (:byrne-line:`blue solid`) bisecting the angle of a triangle, divides the opposite side into segments (:byrne-line:`black solid`, :byrne-line:`black dashed`) proportional to the conterminous sides (:byrne-line:`red solid`, :byrne-line:`yellow solid`). And if a straight line (:byrne-line:`blue solid`) drawn from any angle of a triangle divide the opposite side ((solid-and-dashed black line)) into segments (:byrne-line:`black solid`, :byrne-line:`black dashed`) proportional to the conterminous sides (:byrne-line:`red solid`, :byrne-line:`yellow solid`), it bisects the angle.
