Pickering p. 258: Visual Subtraction of Shared Rectangles
---------------------------------------------------------

On Pickering **p. 258 (Leaf 292)**, Byrne prints the unit square as two vertical color strips (:byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` + :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>`—note Pickering prints yellow on the wider $\phi^{-1}$ strip and blue on the narrower $\phi^{-2}$ strip, whereas our exact symbolic plate preserves 1-to-1 interactive `data-ref` parity between inline glyphs and SVG polygons). Subtracting the shared strip makes the equality of the lower square :byrne-sq:`red outline <vi30:< A E M H >>` and the complementary strip :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>` immediate, whereupon ``VI.14`` converts area equality directly into the continuous Golden Proportion $1 : \phi^{-1} \mathrel{∷} \phi^{-1} : \phi^{-2}$.
