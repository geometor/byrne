Proposition VI.30 — Extreme & Mean Ratio by Application of Areas
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXX. Problem (*Pickering p. 258* · ``G.328``)

*To cut a given finite straight line* (:byrne-line:`red solid-dotted <vi30:/ A E /; / E B />`) *in extreme and mean ratio.*

.. picture:: /_static/figures/vi30.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 30 — Cutting a line in extreme and mean ratio by applying a rectangle exceeding by a square.
   :caption: **Book VI, Prop. XXX (p. 258 · G.328).** Proportional fulfillment of :ref:`II.11 <byrne-ii-11>` via application of areas (``VI.29``).

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Construction (:just:`I.46`, :just:`VI.29`, :just:`I.31`).** On the given straight line (:byrne-line:`red solid <vi30:/ A E />` + :byrne-line:`red dotted <vi30:/ E B />`) describe the unit square (:byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` + :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>`), and to the vertical side apply a rectangle equal to the whole unit square and exceeding by a figure :byrne-sq:`red outline <vi30:< A E M H >>` similar to the square (:just:`VI.29`).

   **Demonstration (:just:`C.N.3`, :just:`VI.14`, :just:`VI.def.3`).**

   Since the applied rectangle (:byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` + :byrne-sq:`red outline <vi30:< A E M H >>`) equals the unit square (:byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` + :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>`), taking away the common part :byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` (:just:`C.N.3`) leaves:

   :byrne-sq:`red outline <vi30:< A E M H >>` = :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>`.

   Because equal equiangular parallelograms have the sides about their equal angles reciprocally proportional (:just:`VI.14`),

   :byrne-op:`therefore` (:byrne-line:`red solid <vi30:/ A E />` + :byrne-line:`red dotted <vi30:/ E B />`) : :byrne-line:`red solid <vi30:/ A E />` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-line:`red solid <vi30:/ A E />` : :byrne-line:`red dotted <vi30:/ E B />`,

   and :byrne-line:`red solid-dotted <vi30:/ A E /; / E B />` is divided in extreme and mean ratio (:just:`VI.def.3`). **Q.E.F.**
