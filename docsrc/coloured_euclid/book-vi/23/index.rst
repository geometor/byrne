Proposition VI.23 — Equiangular Parallelograms Have Ratio Compounded of Sides
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 249*)

EQUIANGULAR parallelograms (:byrne-rect:`yellow filled` and :byrne-rect:`blue filled`) are to one another in a ratio compounded of the ratios of their sides.
