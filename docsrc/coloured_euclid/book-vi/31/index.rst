Proposition VI.31 — Generalized Pythagorean Theorem (Similar Figures)
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXXI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 259*)

IF any similar rectilinear figures be similarly described on the sides of a right angled triangle ((outline triangle with yellow, red, and dotted-and-solid blue sides)), the figure described on the side ((dotted-and-solid blue line)) subtending the right angle is equal to the sum of the figures on the other sides.
