Commentary on Proposition VI.1: Triangles and Parallelograms of Equal Altitude Are as Bases
===========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0246 (pp. 212–213) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.1 <byrne-vi-1>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A triangular fan sharing a common apex and collinear base, made of five alternating black-and-white triangles on the left, a solid red and a solid blue triangle in the middle (resting on blue and red base segments), and three alternating yellow-and-white triangles on the right.
