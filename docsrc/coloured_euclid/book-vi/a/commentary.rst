Commentary on Proposition VI.A: External Angle Bisector Divides Base Externally Proportionally
==============================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0297 (p. 263) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.A <byrne-vi-a>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A triangle on a solid blue base produced rightward as a dotted blue segment, with its black left side produced upward in dotted yellow; a dotted red external-angle bisector meets the produced base, alongside an interior solid red parallel and yellow, blue, red, and black angle sectors.
