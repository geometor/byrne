Proposition VI.A — External Angle Bisector Divides Base Externally Proportionally
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition A. Theorem (*Pickering p. 263*)

IF the right line (:byrne-line:`red dotted`), bisecting an external angle :byrne-angle:`blue` of the triangle (triangle with dotted black, yellow, and solid black sides) meet the opposite side (:byrne-line:`blue solid`) produced, that whole produced side (:byrne-line:`blue solid-dotted`), and its external segment (:byrne-line:`blue dotted`) will be proportional to the sides (:byrne-line:`black solid-dotted` and :byrne-line:`yellow solid`), which contain the angle adjacent to the external bisected angle.
