Commentary on Proposition VI.12: Fourth Proportional to Three Straight Lines
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0264 (p. 230) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.12 <byrne-vi-12>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A triangle formed by an upper blue-and-red side and a lower horizontal yellow-and-black side, crossed by a solid black line and an outer parallel dotted black line.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.31`, :just:`VI.2`, :just:`V.7`.
