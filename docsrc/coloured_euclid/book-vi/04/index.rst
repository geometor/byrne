Proposition VI.4 — Equiangular Triangles Have Proportional Sides
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition IV. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 218–219*)

IN equiangular triangles ((black-and-colored-outline triangle) and (dotted-outline triangle)) the sides about the equal angles are proportional, and the sides which are opposite to the equal angles are homologous.
