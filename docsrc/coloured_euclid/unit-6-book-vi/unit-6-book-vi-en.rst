Book VI Key Definitions (p. 213)
--------------------------------

* **Definition I.** *Similar rectilineal figures* are those which have their several angles equal, each to each, and the sides about the equal angles proportionals (:byrne-op:`prop`).
* **Definition III.** A straight line is said to be *cut in extreme and mean ratio* (:term:`Golden Section <extreme-and-mean-ratio>`) when the whole line is to the greater segment as the greater segment is to the less.

Key Theorems of Similarity (``VI.1``–``VI.13``)
-----------------------------------------------

* **Prop. I–II (``G.286``–``G.287``):** Triangles of the same altitude are to one another as their bases; a line drawn parallel (:byrne-op:`parallel`) to one side of a triangle cuts the other two sides proportionally, and conversely.
* **Prop. III (Angle Bisector Theorem, p. 216 · ``G.288``):** Bisecting an angle of a triangle divides the opposite base into segments proportional to the adjacent sides.
* **Prop. XIII (Mean Proportional · ``G.298``):** Constructing the geometric mean $\sqrt{ab}$ between two segments via Thales's semicircle (``III.31``).

Proposition XXX. Problem (*Extreme & Mean Ratio*, p. 258 · ``G.328``)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

*To cut a given finite straight line* (:byrne-line:`red solid <vi30:/ A E />` + :byrne-line:`red dotted <vi30:/ E B />`) *in extreme and mean ratio.*

.. picture:: /_static/figures/vi30.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 30 — Cutting a line in extreme and mean ratio by applying a rectangle exceeding by a square.
   :caption: **Book VI, Prop. XXX (p. 258).** Proportional fulfillment of ``II.11`` via application of areas (``VI.29``).

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Construction (:just:`I.46`, :just:`VI.29`).** On the given straight line describe the unit square (:byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` + :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>`), and to the vertical side apply a rectangle equal to the whole unit square and exceeding by a figure (:byrne-sq:`red outline <vi30:< A E M H >>`) similar to the square (`VI.29`).

   **Demonstration (:just:`C.N.3`, :just:`VI.14`).**

   Since the applied rectangle (:byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` + :byrne-sq:`red outline <vi30:< A E M H >>`) equals the unit square (:byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` + :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>`), subtracting the common :byrne-rect:`blue filled <vi30:< A E F D >>` yields:

   :byrne-sq:`red outline <vi30:< A E M H >>` = :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <vi30:< E B C F >>`.

   Because equal equiangular parallelograms have reciprocally proportional sides (:just:`VI.14`),

   :byrne-op:`therefore` whole line : :byrne-line:`red solid <vi30:/ A E />` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-line:`red solid <vi30:/ A E />` : :byrne-line:`red dotted <vi30:/ E B />`. **Q.E.F.**
