Commentary on Proposition I.11: Perpendicular from a Point on a Line
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0045 (p. 11) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

Upper right; triangle with blue slanted sides, horizontal black-and-red base (with dotted extensions), and vertical yellow perpendicular dividing the foot into red and blue right-angle sectors.
