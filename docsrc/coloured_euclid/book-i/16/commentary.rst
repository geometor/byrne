Commentary on Proposition I.16: Exterior Angle Exceeds Either Remote Interior
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0050 (p. 16) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.16 <byrne-i-16>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; triangle with dotted yellow left side, dotted blue right side, black base produced as dotted black, red median extended as dotted red to upper-right, and yellow closing segment; black, blue, yellow, red, and outlined external angles.
