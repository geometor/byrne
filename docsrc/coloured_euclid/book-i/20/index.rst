Proposition I.20 — Triangle Inequality
======================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 20*)

Any two sides :byrne-line:`blue solid` and :byrne-line:`red solid` of a triangle (triangle with blue, red, and black sides) taken together are greater than the third side (:byrne-line:`black solid`).
