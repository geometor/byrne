Commentary on Proposition I.43: Equality of Complements in a Parallelogram
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0078 (p. 44) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.43 <byrne-i-43>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; large parallelogram partitioned by a diagonal and two parallel lines into four solid-filled parallelograms: yellow (top-left), blue (top-right), black (bottom-left), and red (bottom-right).
