Proposition I.43 — Equality of Complements in a Parallelogram
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XLIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 44*)

The complements :byrne-rect:`blue filled` and :byrne-rect:`black filled` of the parallelograms which are about the diagonal of a parallelogram are equal.
