Proposition I.18 — Greater Side Subtends Greater Angle
======================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XVIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 18*)

In any triangle (triangle with red/dotted-red, blue, and black sides) if one side (dotted-red and red line) be greater than another :byrne-line:`blue solid`, the angle opposite to the greater side is greater than the angle opposite to the less.
