Proposition I.25 — Converse of the Hinge Theorem
================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 25*)

If two triangles have two sides (:byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`blue solid`) of the one respectively equal to two sides (:byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`blue solid`) of the other, but their bases unequal, the angle subtended by the greater base (:byrne-line:`black solid`) of the one, must be greater than the angle subtended by the less base (:byrne-line:`yellow solid`) of the other.
