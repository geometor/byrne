Proposition I.34 — Opposite Sides, Angles, and Diagonal of a Parallelogram
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXXIV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 35*)

The opposite sides and angles of any parallelogram are equal, and the diagonal (:byrne-line:`black solid`) divides it into two equal parts.
