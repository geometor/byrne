Commentary on Proposition I.34: Opposite Sides, Angles, and Diagonal of a Parallelogram
=======================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0069 (p. 35) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.34 <byrne-i-34>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels.
