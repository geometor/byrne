Proposition I.28 — Exterior or Supplementary Interior Angles Imply Parallels
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXVIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 29*)

If a straight line (:byrne-line:`black solid`), cutting two other straight lines (:byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`yellow solid`), makes the external equal to the internal and opposite angle, at the same side of the cutting line (namely, :byrne-angle:`black` = :byrne-angle:`blue` or :byrne-angle:`yellow` = :byrne-angle:`red`), or if it makes the two internal angles at the same side (:byrne-angle:`red` and :byrne-angle:`blue`, or :byrne-angle:`blue` and :byrne-angle:`red`) together equal to two right angles, those two straight lines are parallel.
