Commentary on Proposition I.9: Bisecting a Rectilineal Angle
============================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0043 (p. 9) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.9 <byrne-i-9>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper right; diamond/kite figure with red upper sides (extended downward as dotted red lines), horizontal yellow chord, blue lower sides, and vertical black bisecting line splitting the top angle into blue and yellow.
