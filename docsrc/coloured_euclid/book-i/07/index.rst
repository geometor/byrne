Proposition I.7 — Uniqueness of Triangle Construction on a Base
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition VII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 7*)

On the same base (:byrne-line:`black solid`), and on the same side of it there cannot be two triangles having their conterminous sides (:byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`red solid`, :byrne-line:`blue solid` and :byrne-line:`blue solid`) at both extremities of the base, equal to each other.
