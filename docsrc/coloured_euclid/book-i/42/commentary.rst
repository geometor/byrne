Commentary on Proposition I.42: Parallelogram Equal to a Given Triangle
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0077 (p. 43) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.42 <byrne-i-42>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper right; yellow parallelogram (dotted red left edge, blue top, red right edge) overlapping a triangle subdivided into blue and black halves along a solid/dotted black base, with a separate yellow angle at upper right.
