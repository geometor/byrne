Proposition I.47 — The Pythagorean Theorem
==========================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XLVII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 48* · ``G.140``)

*In any right-angled triangle, the square which is described upon the side* (:byrne-line:`red solid <i47:/ B C />`) *subtending the right angle is equal to the squares described upon the sides* (:byrne-line:`yellow solid <i47:/ A B />`, :byrne-line:`blue solid <i47:/ A C />`) *which contain the right angle.*

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Given.** Right-angled triangle with hypotenuse :byrne-line:`red solid <i47:/ B C />` subtending the right angle between legs :byrne-line:`yellow solid <i47:/ A B />` and :byrne-line:`blue solid <i47:/ A C />`.

   **Construction.** Describe :byrne-sq:`black filled <i47:< A B F G >>` and :byrne-sq:`red filled <i47:< A C K H >>` on the legs and the hypotenuse square divided by altitude parallel :byrne-line:`black dotted <i47:/ A M />` (:just:`I.46`, :just:`I.31`) into :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <i47:< B L M D >>` and :byrne-rect:`blue filled <i47:< C L M E >>`; draw windmill chords :byrne-line:`black solid <i47:/ A D />` and :byrne-line:`black solid <i47:/ F C />` (:just:`I.post.1`).

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 49

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Demonstration.**

   By collinearity of adjacent right angles (:just:`I.14`), SAS congruence of the rotated windmill triangles (:just:`I.4`), and double-area parallelograms between the same parallels (:just:`I.41`):

   :byrne-sq:`black filled <i47:< A B F G >>` = :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <i47:< B L M D >>`,

   and symmetrically on the right leg (:byrne-line:`blue dotted <i47:/ C K />`, :byrne-line:`red dashed <i47:/ C E />`):

   :byrne-sq:`red filled <i47:< A C K H >>` = :byrne-rect:`blue filled <i47:< C L M E >>`.

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <i47:< B L M D >>` + :byrne-rect:`blue filled <i47:< C L M E >>` = :byrne-sq:`black filled <i47:< A B F G >>` + :byrne-sq:`red filled <i47:< A C K H >>` (:just:`C.N.2`). **Q.E.D.**
