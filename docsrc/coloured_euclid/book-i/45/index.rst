Proposition I.45 — Parallelogram Equal to Any Rectilineal Figure
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XLV. Problem (*Pickering p. 46*)

To construct a parallelogram equal to a given rectilinear figure ((red-yellow-blue pentagon)) and having an angle equal to a given rectilinear angle (:byrne-angle:`red`).

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 80 (p. 46)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Left side; upper pentagon subdivided into red, yellow, and blue triangles; lower composite parallelogram composed of adjacent blue, yellow, and red parallelograms with yellow, black, and blue base angle sectors.
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** :just:`I.42`, :just:`I.44`, ``prs. 29, 14, 30``
