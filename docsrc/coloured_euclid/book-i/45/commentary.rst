Commentary on Proposition I.45: Parallelogram Equal to Any Rectilineal Figure
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0080 (p. 46) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.45 <byrne-i-45>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Left side; upper pentagon subdivided into red, yellow, and blue triangles; lower composite parallelogram composed of adjacent blue, yellow, and red parallelograms with yellow, black, and blue base angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.42`, :just:`I.44`, ``prs. 29, 14, 30``.
