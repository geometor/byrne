Proposition I.36 — Parallelograms on Equal Bases and Parallels
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXXVI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 37*)

Parallelograms (:byrne-rect:`red filled` and :byrne-rect:`yellow filled`) on equal bases, and between the same parallels, are equal.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 71 (p. 37)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Upper right; two filled parallelograms (red left, yellow right) bridged by a central slanted parallelogram (blue interior with red and yellow triangular ends, yellow diagonal, and dotted black edge) on equal black and blue bases.
