Commentary on Proposition I.36: Parallelograms on Equal Bases and Parallels
===========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0071 (p. 37) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.36 <byrne-i-36>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper right; two filled parallelograms (red left, yellow right) bridged by a central slanted parallelogram (blue interior with red and yellow triangular ends, yellow diagonal, and dotted black edge) on equal black and blue bases.
