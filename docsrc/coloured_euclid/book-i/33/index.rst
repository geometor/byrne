Proposition I.33 — Lines Joining Equal and Parallel Extremities
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXXIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 34*)

Straight lines (:byrne-line:`blue solid` and :byrne-line:`yellow solid`) which join the adjacent extremities of two equal and parallel straight lines (:byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`red dotted`), are themselves equal and parallel.
