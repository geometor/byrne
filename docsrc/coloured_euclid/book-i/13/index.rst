Proposition I.13 — Adjacent Angles Equal Two Right Angles
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 13*)

When a straight line (:byrne-line:`yellow solid`) standing upon another straight line (:byrne-line:`red solid`) makes angles with it; they are either two right angles or together equal to two right angles.
