Proposition I.24 — Hinge Theorem (Greater Contained Angle)
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXIV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 24*)

If two triangles have two sides of the one respectively equal to two sides of the other (:byrne-line:`blue solid` to :byrne-line:`blue solid` and :byrne-line:`red dotted` to :byrne-line:`red solid`), and if one of the angles ((triangle with hatched top angle)) contained by the equal sides be greater than the other ((triangle with dotted arc at top angle)), the side (:byrne-line:`black solid`) which is opposite to the greater angle is greater than the side (:byrne-line:`yellow solid`) which is opposite to the less angle.
