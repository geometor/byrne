Proposition I.44 — Applying a Parallelogram Equal to a Triangle
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XLIV. Problem (*Pickering p. 45*)

To a given straight line (:byrne-line:`black solid`) to apply a parallelogram equal to a given triangle (:byrne-tri:`red filled`), and having an angle equal to a given rectilinear angle (:byrne-angle:`yellow`).
