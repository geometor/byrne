Proposition I.38 — Triangles on Equal Bases and Parallels
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXXVIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 39*)

Triangles (:byrne-tri:`red filled` and :byrne-tri:`blue filled`) on equal bases and between :byrne-line:`black dotted` the same parallels are equal.

----------------------------------------
