Commentary on Proposition V.11: Equivalence Relation of Eudoxian Ratios Across Heterogeneous Kinds
==================================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0209 (p. 175) & Trichotomy Composition (V.11 · G.271)

Six Magnitude Tokens Across Three Homogeneous Ranks
---------------------------------------------------

On Pickering **p. 175 (Leaf 209)**, Byrne pairs magnitudes by shape-color rank (:byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`blue square`, :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow shield`, and small black tokens :byrne-mag:`black pentagon` : :byrne-mag:`black circle`). Critically, ``V.11`` requires homogeneity *only within each ratio pair*, NOT across the three pairs—allowing a ratio of two *areas* (say in Book VI) to equal a ratio of two *line segments* via a common mediating ratio!
