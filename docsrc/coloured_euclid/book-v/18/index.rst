Proposition V.18 — Componendo (Joint Proportion)
================================================

.. rubric:: Book V, Proposition XVIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 185* · ``G.278``)

*If magnitudes, taken separately, be proportionals, they shall also be proportionals when taken jointly: that is, if the first be to the second as the third is to the fourth, the first and second together shall be to the second as the third and fourth together is to the fourth.*


.. picture:: /_static/figures/v18.svg
   :alt: Book V Proposition 18 — Chiswick Press colored magnitude tableau for Componendo.
   :caption: **Pickering p. 185 Tableau (*Componendo*).** Composing antecedent and consequent colored tokens jointly.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Given.** Let :byrne-mag:`red shield` : :byrne-mag:`black shield` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`yellow square` : :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`;

   then (:byrne-mag:`red shield` + :byrne-mag:`black shield`) : :byrne-mag:`black shield` :byrne-op:`prop` (:byrne-mag:`yellow square` + :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`) : :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`.

   **Demonstration (*Reductio* via :just:`V.17`, :just:`V.11`, :just:`V.9`).**

   For if not, let (:byrne-mag:`red shield` + :byrne-mag:`black shield`) : :byrne-mag:`black shield` :byrne-op:`prop` (:byrne-mag:`yellow square` + :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`) : :byrne-mag:`black circle`, supposing :byrne-mag:`black circle` $\neq$ :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`;

   :byrne-op:`therefore` *dividendo* (:just:`V.17`) :byrne-mag:`red shield` : :byrne-mag:`black shield` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`yellow square` : :byrne-mag:`black circle` (after separating the fourth term);

   but :byrne-mag:`red shield` : :byrne-mag:`black shield` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`yellow square` : :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` (hyp.),

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-mag:`yellow square` : :byrne-mag:`black circle` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`yellow square` : :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` (:just:`V.11`),

   whence :byrne-mag:`black circle` = :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` (:just:`V.9`), contrary to supposition!

   :byrne-op:`therefore` (:byrne-mag:`red shield` + :byrne-mag:`black shield`) : :byrne-mag:`black shield` :byrne-op:`prop` (:byrne-mag:`yellow square` + :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`) : :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`. **Q.E.D.**
