Book V Definition V — Criterion of Same Ratio
=============================================

.. rubric:: Eudoxus's Equimultiple Criterion in Coloured Magnitude Arrays (*Pickering pp. 151–154, Leaves 185–188*)

.. picture:: 0185.g1.png
   :alt: Book V Definition V colored equimultiple arrays of red circles, blue diamonds, yellow squares, and black shields (Leaf 185, p. 151).
   :caption: **Book V, Definition V Archival Plate (Leaf 185, p. 151).** Byrne's staircase equimultiple arrays introducing typed magnitude registers.

.. picture:: 0185.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram of Book V Definition V Eudoxian Criterion.
   :caption: **Calibrated Vector Tableau (0185.g1).** :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`black shield`.

Four magnitudes (:byrne-mag:`red circle`, :byrne-mag:`yellow square`, :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`, :byrne-mag:`black shield`) are said to be **proportionals**, or in the **same ratio**, when any equimultiples whatsoever be taken of the first and third, and any equimultiples whatsoever of the second and fourth: if the multiple of the first be greater than (:byrne-op:`gt`), equal to (``=``), or less than (:byrne-op:`lt`) the multiple of the second, then the multiple of the third is also greater than, equal to, or less than that of the fourth (:just:`V.def.5`).
