Book V Definition VII — Criterion of Greater Ratio
==================================================

.. rubric:: Witness Multiples for Ratio Inequality (*Pickering pp. 166–168, Leaves 200–202*)

When of the equimultiples of four magnitudes (taken as in :ref:`Definition V <byrne-v-def-5>`), the multiple of the first is greater (:byrne-op:`gt`) than that of the second, while the multiple of the third is *not* greater than that of the fourth, then the first is said to have to the second a **greater ratio** than the third magnitude has to the fourth (:just:`V.def.7`).
