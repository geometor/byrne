Commentary on Book V Definitions I–IV (Part, Multiple & Ratio)
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Leaf 179 (p. 145)

Byrne interleaves Book V's technical definitions directly adjacent to the theorems that invoke them, turning Euclid's prose nomenclature (*Alternando*, *Dividendo*, *Componendo*, *Ex æquali*, *Duplicate/Compound Ratio*) into an inline visual-symbolic toolkit.
