Book V Definition XV — Componendo
=================================

.. rubric:: Proportion by Composition Nomenclature (*Pickering p. 184, Leaf 218*)

*Componendo* (by composition) is used when there are four proportionals, and it is inferred that the first together with the second is to the second as the third together with the fourth is to the fourth (:ref:`Prop. V.18 <byrne-v-18>`). Note that Byrne prints Def. XV immediately prior to ``V.18``.
