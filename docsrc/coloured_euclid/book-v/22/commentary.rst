Commentary on Proposition V.22: Compound Ratio Cancellation & Inductive Rank Chains
===================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folios 0227–0228 (pp. 193–194) & Semigroup Composition (V.22 · G.282)

Telescoping Proportional Chains on Pickering pp. 193–194
--------------------------------------------------------

On Pickering **pp. 193–194 (Leaves 227–228)**, Byrne proves the three-term base case via three independent integer multipliers $(M, m, N)$ and ``V.20``, and then explicitly steps up on **p. 194** to four-magnitude ranks ("and so on, whatever the number of magnitudes be"). Algebraically, *ex aequali* (`G.282`) is the associative composition law of Eudoxian ratios $(a:b)(b:c) = (a:c)$—the foundational engine of compound ratios in Book VI (`VI.23`) and Menelaus/Ceva/Pappus projective invariants (`geometor/pappus`, `geometor/menelaus`, `geometor/ceva`).
