Book V Definition XVI — Dividendo
=================================

.. rubric:: Proportion by Division Nomenclature (*Pickering p. 182, Leaf 216*)

*Dividendo* (by division) is used when there are four proportionals, and it is inferred that the excess of the first above the second is to the second as the excess of the third above the fourth is to the fourth (:ref:`Prop. V.17 <byrne-v-17>`). Note that Byrne prints Def. XVI immediately prior to ``V.17``.
