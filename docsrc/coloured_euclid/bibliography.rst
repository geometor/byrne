Systematic Bibliography
=======================

.. rubric:: Primary Euclidean Editions, Chiswick Press Scholarship & Visual Information Design

.. _cite-byrne-1847:

**Oliver Byrne** — *The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid in Which Coloured Diagrams and Symbols Are Used Instead of Letters for the Greater Ease of Learners*
   London: William Pickering (printed by Charles Whittingham at the Chiswick Press), 1847. Public-domain witness digitized by the Thomas Fisher Rare Book Library, University of Toronto (Internet Archive ``firstsixbooksofe00eucl``).

   Primary source of this Digital Scriptorium Codex, visually verified leaf-by-leaf for colour-plate assignments, Simson axiom numbering, and formal operator typography.

.. _cite-simson-1756:

**Robert Simson** — *The Elements of Euclid, viz. the First Six Books, together with the Eleventh and Twelfth*
   Glasgow: Printed by Robert and Andrew Foulis, 1756.

   Standard 18th- and 19th-century British Euclidean text whose English wording and numbering (35 Definitions, 3 Postulates, 12 Axioms) Oliver Byrne adopted throughout his 1847 edition.

.. _cite-horace-ars:

**Horace (Quintus Horatius Flaccus)** — *Ars Poetica (Epistula ad Pisones)*
   Lines 180–182, Classical Latin Corpus, c. 19 BC.

   Quoted by Oliver Byrne in the 1847 *Introduction* (p. viii) to defend ocular demonstration over verbal/auditory exposition.

.. _cite-heath-1908:

**Sir Thomas Little Heath** — *The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements, Translated from the Text of Heiberg with Introduction and Commentary*
   3 vols., Cambridge University Press, 1908 (2nd ed. 1926).

   Canonical alphabetic translation digitized in ``geometor/euclid``, paired alongside Byrne's 1847 visual notation in our 3-way Rosetta analysis.

.. _cite-proclus-friedlein:

**Proclus Diadochus** — *In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii*
   Ed. G. Friedlein (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1873); English trans. G. R. Morrow, *A Commentary on the First Book of Euclid's Elements* (Princeton University Press, 1970).

   Defines the six formal parts of a Euclidean proposition (*protasis*, *ekthesis*, *diorismos*, *kataskeue*, *apodeixis*, *symperasma*) digitized in ``geometor/proclus``.

.. _cite-hoenen-1954:

**Petrus Hubertus Jacobus Hoenen, S.J.** — *De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis*
   Analecta Gregoriana, Vol. LXIII, Apud Aedes Universitatis Gregorianae, Romae, 1954.

   Demonstrates how universal geometric necessity is read directly within the concrete spatial *phantasma* (``geometor/hoenen``)—the epistemological foundation of Byrne's visual tokens.

.. _cite-demorgan-1872:

**Augustus De Morgan** — *A Budget of Paradoxes*
   London: Longmans, Green, and Co., 1872.

   Documents Victorian academic resistance to Byrne's chromatic reform of Euclidean pedagogy.

.. _cite-mclean-1963:

**Ruari McLean** — *Victorian Book Design and Colour Printing*
   London: Faber & Faber, 1963 (2nd enlarged ed., 1972).

   Landmark bibliographic history rehabilitating Oliver Byrne and Charles Whittingham's four-run relief printing at the Chiswick Press.

.. _cite-tufte-1990:

**Edward R. Tufte** — *Envisioning Information*
   Cheshire, Connecticut: Graphics Press, 1990, pp. 84–87.

   Celebrated information-design analysis showing how Byrne's primary colors eliminate indirect alphabetic label scanning between text and figure.

.. _cite-hawcock-2010:

**Werner Oechslin (Introduction)** — *Oliver Byrne: Six Books of Euclid (Facsimile Edition)*
   Cologne: Taschen, 2010.

   Full-scale photographic facsimile of the 1847 Pickering print that reintroduced Byrne's work to contemporary designers and mathematicians.

.. _cite-rougeux-2018:

**Nicholas Rougeux** — *Byrne's Euclid: The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid with Coloured Diagrams and Symbols*
   Interactive web edition, ``https://www.c82.net/euclid/``, December 2018 (licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

   Pioneering digital humanities tribute featuring interactive shape-highlighting and restored Caslon woodcut initials. Cited here in scholarly appreciation; zero code, CSS, SVG, or graphic assets from ``c82.net`` are copied in ``geometor/byrne``.

.. _cite-rougeux-2018-making:

**Nicholas Rougeux** — *Making of Byrne's Euclid*
   Design and engineering essay, ``https://www.c82.net/work/?id=367``, December 2018.

   Valuable contemporary account of re-typesetting all 269 pages of Byrne's 1847 treatise and cataloguing subtle Chiswick Press colour-registration anomalies.
