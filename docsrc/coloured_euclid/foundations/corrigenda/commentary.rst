Commentary on the 1847 Corrigenda
=================================

.. rubric:: Leaf 33 (p. xxix) · Four-Press Registration Sensitivity

Seven of Byrne's eleven errata stem directly from colour-plate woodblock substitutions (e.g., black vs. blue on pp. 54, 59, 81, 141) or an omitted operator/coefficient during Chiswick Press multi-pass imposition—highlighting how unforgiving a pass-by-value visual notation becomes if a single press block shifts ink bed!
