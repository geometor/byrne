Commentary on Book I Definitions (Leaves 22–26)
===============================================

.. rubric:: Simson 1756 Numbering vs. Critical Heiberg Text

Because :person:`Byrne <byrne>` adopts :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` expanded classification of four-sided figures, *parallel straight lines* stands at **Def. XXXV** (Heiberg/Heath ``I.def.23``) and *square* at **Def. XXVII** (Heath ``I.def.22``). See :ref:`Concordance <concordance>`.
