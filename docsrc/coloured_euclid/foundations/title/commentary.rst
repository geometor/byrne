Commentary on the 1847 Title Page & Dedication
==============================================

.. rubric:: Archival Witness: Leaves 5, 7 & 9 (Pickering 1847)

On **Leaf 7**, :person:`Whittingham's <whittingham>` pressman inverted the yellow and blue ink runs on the divided hypotenuse square relative to the canonical proof plate on **pp. 48–49 (Leaves 82–83)**—printing the narrow left strip in Prussian blue and the wide right rectangle in ochre yellow. Both the full-RGB plate ``0007.g1.png`` and calibrated vector diagram ``0007.g1.svg`` preserve this 1847 typographic curiosity verbatim.
