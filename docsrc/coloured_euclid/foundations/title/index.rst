Title Page & Dedication
=======================

.. rubric:: Half-Title, Polychrome Title Page & Dedication to Earl Fitzwilliam (Leaves 5–9)

.. folio:: 0005
   :page: half-title

**Half-Title (Leaf 5):** *BYRNE'S EUCLID. THE FIRST SIX BOOKS OF THE ELEMENTS OF EUCLID, IN WHICH COLOURED DIAGRAMS AND SYMBOLS ARE USED INSTEAD OF LETTERS FOR THE GREATER EASE OF LEARNERS.*

**Title Page (Leaf 7):** Printed by :person:`Charles Whittingham <whittingham>` at the Chiswick Press for :person:`William Pickering <pickering>`, London, 1847, emblazoned with Byrne's signature four-color Pythagorean Windmill theorem emblem.

.. picture:: 0007.g1.png
   :alt: Oliver Byrne 1847 Title Page polychrome Pythagorean Windmill woodblock emblem (Leaf 7).
   :caption: **1847 Title Page Archival Plate (Leaf 7).** Full-RGB Chiswick Press relief emblem showing the transposed yellow/blue hypotenuse rectangles.

.. picture:: 0007.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram of Byrne's 1847 Title Page Pythagorean emblem preserving the yellow/blue transposition.
   :caption: **Calibrated Vector Emblem (0007.g1).** Theme-adaptive 5-layer ``.euclid-graphic`` faithful to Leaf 7 (blue left strip, yellow right rectangle).

.. folio:: 0009
   :page: dedication

**Dedication (Leaf 9):** *"To the Right Honorable Charles William, Earl Fitzwilliam... This Work is Respectfully Inscribed by His Lordship's Obliged and Grateful Servant, Oliver Byrne."*
