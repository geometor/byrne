Preliminaries — Introduction, Definitions, Postulates, Axioms & Symbols
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Oliver Byrne — *Pickering Edition* (pp. i–xxix, Leaves 5–33)

:person:`Oliver Byrne's <byrne>` 1847 preliminary apparatus spans leaves ``0005``–``0033``: half-title, main polychrome title page, dedication to Earl Fitzwilliam, the ocular pedagogy *Introduction* (pp. vii–xvii), :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` 35 Book I Definitions, 3 Postulates, and 12 Axioms, Byrne's *Elucidations*, complete operator *Symbols and Abbreviations*, and *Corrigenda*.
