Byrne's Radical Leap in Book V: Abstract Colored Magnitude Tokens (p. 154)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

In Book V, :person:`Eudoxus <eudoxus>` and :person:`Euclid <euclid>` generalize ratio and proportion from spatial lines to *arbitrary continuous magnitudes of the same kind*—lengths, planar areas, polyhedral volumes, angles, or time intervals. Recognizing that line diagrams obscure this universality, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` abandons spatial figures altogether across pages ``153–212`` and introduces **four typed colored magnitude symbols**:

* First magnitude: :byrne-mag:`red circle` (``:byrne-mag:`red circle```)
* Second magnitude: :byrne-mag:`yellow square` (``:byrne-mag:`yellow square```)
* Third magnitude: :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` (``:byrne-mag:`blue diamond```)
* Fourth magnitude: :byrne-mag:`black shield` (``:byrne-mag:`black shield```)

**Definition V (:just:`V.def.5` · Eudoxus's Criterion of Same Ratio, p. 154)**

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   Four magnitudes are in the same ratio,

   :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`black shield`,

   when for *every* pair of positive integer equimultiples $m, n \in \mathbb{N}^+$:

   * if $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`red circle` :byrne-op:`gt` $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`yellow square`, then $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` :byrne-op:`gt` $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`black shield`;
   * if $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`red circle` = $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`yellow square`, then $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` = $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`black shield`; and
   * if $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`red circle` :byrne-op:`lt` $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`yellow square`, then $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` :byrne-op:`lt` $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`black shield`.

Core Laws of Colored Ratio Algebra (``V.11``, ``V.16``, ``V.18``, ``V.22``)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Proposition XI (Transitivity of Ratios · ``G.271``).**
   If :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`black shield` and :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`black shield` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`red pentagon` : :byrne-mag:`yellow circle`,
   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`red pentagon` : :byrne-mag:`yellow circle`. **Q.E.D.**

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Proposition XVI (:term:`Alternando <alternando>`, p. 187 · ``G.276``).**
   *If four magnitudes of the same kind be proportionals, they shall also be proportionals when taken alternately.*

   **Given.** :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`black shield`.

   **Demonstration (:just:`V.15`, :just:`V.11`, :just:`V.14`, :just:`V.def.5`).**
   For arbitrary equimultiples $m, n$:

   $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`red circle` : $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`black shield`.

   By ``V.14``, according as $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`red circle` is :byrne-op:`gt`, ``=``, or :byrne-op:`lt` $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`, so is $m \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`gt`, ``=``, or :byrne-op:`lt` $n \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`black shield`.

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`yellow square` : :byrne-mag:`black shield`. **Q.E.D.**

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Proposition XVIII (*Componendo* · ``G.278``).**
   If :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`black shield`,
   :byrne-op:`therefore` (:byrne-mag:`red circle` + :byrne-mag:`yellow square`) : :byrne-mag:`yellow square` :byrne-op:`prop` (:byrne-mag:`blue diamond` + :byrne-mag:`black shield`) : :byrne-mag:`black shield`. **Q.E.D.**

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Proposition XXII (*Ex Aequali* · ``G.282``).**
   If (:byrne-mag:`red circle`, :byrne-mag:`yellow square`, :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`) and (:byrne-mag:`red pentagon`, :byrne-mag:`yellow circle`, :byrne-mag:`black shield`) have pairwise equal ratios,
   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`red pentagon` : :byrne-mag:`black shield`. **Q.E.D.**
