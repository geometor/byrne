Unit V Summary: Eudoxian Proportion as Typed Colored Algebra
============================================================

.. rubric:: Why Book V Is Byrne's Most Modern Algebraic Innovation

1. **Redundant Shape + Color Dual Coding**:
   Byrne pairs four distinct **shapes** (*circle*, *square*, *diamond*, *shield*) with his four **colors** (*red*, *yellow*, *blue*, *black*). Even for color-deficient readers or in dim light, every term of a four-part proportion retains a unique geometric silhouette.
2. **Elimination of False Metric Intuition**:
   Classical editions draw four line segments of arbitrary fixed lengths for Book V propositions, tempting students to assume commensurability. Byrne's abstract tokens (:byrne-mag:`red circle`, :byrne-mag:`yellow square`, :byrne-mag:`blue diamond`, :byrne-mag:`black shield`) faithfully preserve Eudoxus's purely relational axioms across commensurable and irrational magnitudes alike ($\sqrt{2}$, $\phi$).
3. **Verbal Compression Record (92.9% Reduction)**:
   In ``V.16`` (*Alternando*), Heath's prose requires 253 words and 20 letter lookups (`A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H`), whereas Byrne's equational layout uses just 18 connecting words and zero auxiliary letter names by writing integer multipliers ($2 \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`red circle`, $3 \cdot$ :byrne-mag:`red circle`) directly!
