Proposition II.2 — Sum of Rectangles on Parts Equals Square
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition II. Theorem (*Pickering p. 54*)

IF a straight line be divided into any two parts (blue-red line), the square of the whole line is equal to the sum of the rectangles contained by the whole line and each of its parts. (blue-red line)² = { (blue-red line) · :byrne-line:`blue solid` + (blue-red line) · :byrne-line:`red solid` }.
