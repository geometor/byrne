Proposition II.12 — Obtuse-Triangle Law of Cosines
==================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition XII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 67*)

IN any obtuse angled triangle, the square of the side subtending the obtuse angle exceeds the sum of the squares of the sides containing the obtuse angle, by twice the rectangle contained by either of these sides and the produced parts of the same from the obtuse angle to the perpendicular let fall on it from the opposite acute angle. :byrne-line:`blue solid squared` ⊐ :byrne-line:`black solid squared` + :byrne-line:`red solid squared` by 2 :byrne-line:`black solid` · (black-dotted line).
