Proposition II.4 — Binomial Square
==================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition IV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 56* · ``G.147``)

*If a straight line be divided into any two parts* (:byrne-line:`blue solid <ii4:/ A C />` *and* :byrne-line:`red solid <ii4:/ C B />`), *the square on the whole line is equal to the squares on the two parts, together with twice the rectangle contained by the parts.*

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Construction.** Describe the square on the whole line (:just:`I.46`), draw diagonal :byrne-line:`black solid <ii4:/ D G />` + :byrne-line:`black dotted <ii4:/ G B />` (:just:`I.post.1`), and through the division point draw parallels partitioning the square into four sub-regions (:just:`I.31`).

   **Demonstration (:just:`I.29`, :just:`I.6`, :just:`I.34`, :just:`I.43`).**

   Along diagonal :byrne-line:`black solid <ii4:/ D G />` + :byrne-line:`black dotted <ii4:/ G B />`, isosceles base-angle transfer (:just:`I.29`, :just:`I.6`) proves that the two parallelograms about the diameter are equilateral right-angled squares:

   :byrne-sq:`blue half <ii4:< D K G H >>` = :byrne-line:`blue solid squared <ii4:/ A C />` and :byrne-sq:`red half <ii4:< G F B C >>` = :byrne-line:`red solid squared <ii4:/ C B />`,

   while the two complements :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii4:< H G C A >>` and :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii4:< K E F G >>` are equal (:just:`I.43`) and each equals :byrne-line:`blue solid <ii4:/ A C />` :byrne-op:`rect-by` :byrne-line:`red solid <ii4:/ C B />`.

   :byrne-op:`therefore` whole square = :byrne-sq:`blue half <ii4:< D K G H >>` + :byrne-sq:`red half <ii4:< G F B C >>` + 2 × :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii4:< H G C A >>`. **Q.E.D.**
