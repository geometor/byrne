Proposition II.3 — Rectangle on Whole and Part Equals Square Plus Part Rectangle
================================================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition III. Theorem (*Pickering p. 55*)

IF a straight line be divided into any two parts (blue-red line), the rectangle contained by the whole line and either of its parts, is equal to the square of that part, together with the rectangle under the parts. (blue-red line) · :byrne-line:`blue solid` = :byrne-line:`blue solid squared` + :byrne-line:`blue solid` · :byrne-line:`red solid`, or, (blue-red line) · :byrne-line:`red solid` = :byrne-line:`red solid squared` + :byrne-line:`blue solid` · :byrne-line:`red solid`.
