Proposition II.9 — Sum of Squares of Equal and Unequal Segments
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition IX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 63*)

IF a straight line be divided into two equal parts (blue-yellow-red line), and also into two unequal parts (blue-yellow-red line), the squares of the unequal parts are together double the squares of half the line, and of the part between the points of section. (blue-yellow line)² + :byrne-line:`red solid squared` = 2 :byrne-line:`blue solid squared` + 2 :byrne-line:`yellow solid squared`.
