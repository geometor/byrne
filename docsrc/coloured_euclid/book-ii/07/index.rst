Proposition II.7 — Square on Whole Plus Square on Part
======================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition VII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 61*)

IF a straight line be divided into any two parts (blue-red line), the squares of the whole line and one of the parts are equal to twice the rectangle contained by the whole line and that part, together with the square of the other parts. (blue-red line)² + :byrne-line:`red solid squared` = 2 (blue-red line) · :byrne-line:`red solid` + :byrne-line:`blue solid squared`
