Commentary on Proposition II.7: Square on Whole Plus Square on Part
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0095 (p. 61) · Book II Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition II.7 <byrne-ii-7>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Large square divided by a solid black diagonal and black-dotted lines into a large lower-left solid red square, a small upper-right solid blue square, an upper-left yellow rectangle, and a lower-right solid black rectangle.
