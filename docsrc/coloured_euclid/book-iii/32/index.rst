Proposition III.32 — Alternate Segment Theorem (Tangent-Chord Angle)
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXXII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 117*)

If a right line :byrne-line:`blue solid` be a tangent to a circle, and from the point of contact a right line :byrne-line:`red solid` be drawn cutting the circle, the angle :byrne-angle:`yellow` made by this line with the tangent is equal to the angle :byrne-angle:`yellow` in the alternate segment of the circle.
