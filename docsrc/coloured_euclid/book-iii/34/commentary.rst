Commentary on Proposition III.34: Cutting Off a Segment Containing a Given Angle
================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0153 (p. 119) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.34 <byrne-iii-34>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue circle with horizontal red tangent at bottom, black chord cutting off a yellow-filled segment on the right, and a blue angle sector between chord and tangent.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.17`, :just:`III.32`.
