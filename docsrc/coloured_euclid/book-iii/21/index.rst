Proposition III.21 — Angles in the Same Segment Are Equal
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 104*)

The angles (:byrne-angle:`red`, :byrne-angle:`blue`) in the same segment of a circle are equal.
