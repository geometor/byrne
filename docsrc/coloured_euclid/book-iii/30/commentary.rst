Commentary on Proposition III.30: Bisecting a Given Circumference
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0148 (p. 114) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.30 <byrne-iii-30>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Circular arc (solid red left, dotted red right) on a solid+dotted black chord, vertical yellow perpendicular bisector with blue and red right-angle sectors, and dotted blue and solid blue chords to the arc midpoint.
