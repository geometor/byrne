Commentary on Proposition III.12: Line of Centres Passes Through External Contact
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0123 (p. 89) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.12 <byrne-iii-12>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue and red circles touching externally, with horizontal red-black-blue line joining centers and dotted yellow and solid yellow radii to the point of contact.
