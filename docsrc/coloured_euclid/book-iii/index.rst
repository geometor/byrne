Book III — Circles, Inscribed Angles & Tangent-Secant Power
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Cyclic Angles, Semicircular Right Angles & Power of a Point (*Pickering pp. 71–122, Leaves 105–156*)

.. folio:: 0105
   :page: 71

Book III investigates the incidence and angular geometry of circles, culminating in three foundational theorems rendered with crystalline economy in Byrne's four-color notation: central-to-inscribed angle doubling (:ref:`III.20 <byrne-iii-20>`), Thales's semicircle theorem (:ref:`III.31 <byrne-iii-31>`), and the tangent-secant power of an external point (:ref:`III.36 <byrne-iii-36>`).

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iii31.svg
   :alt: Book III Featured Plate — Angle in a semicircle is a right angle (Proposition III.31).
   :caption: **Book III Showcase — Proposition III.31 (p. 115 · G.196).** Partitioning the angle in a semicircle by a radius into two isosceles colored angle pairs proves that every semicircular angle equals one right angle.
