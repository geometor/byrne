Book III Summary: Cyclic Angles & Power of a Point
==================================================

.. rubric:: How Book I Triangle Laws & Book II Algebra Unlock the Circle

.. folio:: 0105
   :page: 71

1. **Inscribed & Semicircular Angles (:ref:`III.20 <byrne-iii-20>`, :ref:`III.31 <byrne-iii-31>`)**:
   Combining isosceles radius triangles (:ref:`I.5 <byrne-i-5>`) with the exterior-angle theorem (:ref:`I.32 <byrne-i-32>`) proves that central angles double inscribed angles (:ref:`III.20 <byrne-iii-20>`) and that every angle inscribed in a semicircle is a right angle (:ref:`III.31 <byrne-iii-31>`).
2. **Tangent-Secant Power (:ref:`III.36 <byrne-iii-36>`)**:
   Uniting perpendicular tangent contact (``III.18``), Book II geometric algebra (``II.6``), and the Pythagorean theorem (:ref:`I.47 <byrne-i-47>`) establishes that the square on a tangent equals the rectangle contained by any secant and its external segment—the exact lemma whose converse (``III.37``) powers the Golden Triangle construction in :ref:`IV.10 <byrne-iv-10>`.
