Proposition III.20 — Central & Inscribed Angles
===============================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 102* · ``G.185``)

*The angle at the centre of a circle is double the angle at the circumference, when they have the same part of the circumference for their base.*

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iii20.svg
   :alt: Book III Proposition 20 — Blue circle with central blue angle double the inscribed yellow angle.
   :caption: **Book III, Prop. XX (p. 102 · G.185).** The angle at the centre :byrne-angle:`blue <iii20:< B O C )>` is double the angle at the circumference :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>`.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Case I (Centre on a side of the angle).** Let the centre of :byrne-circle:`blue <iii20:( O A )>` lie on diameter :byrne-line:`red solid-dotted <iii20:/ A O /; / O D />`, a side of :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>`.

   Because radii :byrne-line:`black solid <iii20:/ O B />` = :byrne-line:`red solid <iii20:/ A O />` (:just:`I.def.15`),

   :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>` = :byrne-angle:`red <iii20:< O B A )>` (:just:`I.5`).

   But exterior central angle :byrne-angle:`blue <iii20:< B O C )>` = :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>` + :byrne-angle:`red <iii20:< O B A )>` (:just:`I.32`),

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-angle:`blue <iii20:< B O C )>` = twice :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>`. (And similarly by sum or difference of diameters when the centre lies within or without the angle.) **Q.E.D.**
