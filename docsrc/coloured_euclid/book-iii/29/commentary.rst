Commentary on Proposition III.29: Equal Arcs Are Subtended by Equal Chords
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0147 (p. 113) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.29 <byrne-iii-29>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two equal circles: top yellow circle with blue lower arc, solid red chord, black and blue radii, and red central angle; bottom black circle with red lower arc, dotted red chord, dotted black and dotted blue radii, and yellow central angle.
