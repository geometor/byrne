Proposition III.29 — Equal Arcs Are Subtended by Equal Chords
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXIX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 113*)

In equal circles :byrne-circle:`yellow` and :byrne-circle:`black` the chords :byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`red dotted` which subtend equal arcs are equal.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 147 (p. 113)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Two equal circles: top yellow circle with blue lower arc, solid red chord, black and blue radii, and red central angle; bottom black circle with red lower arc, dotted red chord, dotted black and dotted blue radii, and yellow central angle.
