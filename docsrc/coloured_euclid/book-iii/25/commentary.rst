Commentary on Proposition III.25: Completing a Circle from a Given Segment
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0142 (p. 108) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.25 <byrne-iii-25>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue circular arc with blue and black chords drawn from a point on the arc, and yellow and red perpendicular bisectors meeting at the center.
