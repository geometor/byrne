Proposition III.17 — Drawing a Tangent from an Exterior Point
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XVII. Problem (*Pickering pp. 98–99*)

To draw a tangent to a given circle :byrne-circle:`red` from a given point, either in or outside of its circumference.
