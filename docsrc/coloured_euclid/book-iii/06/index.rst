Proposition III.6 — Internally Touching Circles Are Not Concentric
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition VI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 79*)

If two circles (black circle inside red circle touching internally) touch one another internally, they have not the same centre.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 113 (p. 79)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Red outer circle and black inner circle touching at top, yellow radius to contact point, and dotted+solid blue line from supposed common center cutting both circles.
