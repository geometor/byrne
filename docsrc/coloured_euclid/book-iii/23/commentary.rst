Commentary on Proposition III.23: Similar Unequal Segments Cannot Stand on Same Chord
=====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0140 (p. 106) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.23 <byrne-iii-23>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue and red circular arcs on a common black horizontal base on the same side, with yellow, blue, and red chords and blue and yellow angle sectors.
