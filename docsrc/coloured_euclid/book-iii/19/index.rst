Proposition III.19 — Perpendicular to Tangent Passes Through Centre
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XIX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 101*)

If a straight line :byrne-line:`blue solid` be a tangent to a circle, the straight line :byrne-line:`yellow solid`, drawn perpendicular to it from point of the contact, passes through the centre of the circle.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 135 (p. 101)
