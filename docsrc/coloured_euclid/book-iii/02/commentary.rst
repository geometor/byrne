Commentary on Proposition III.2: Chord Lies Wholly Within the Circle
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0109 (p. 75) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.2 <byrne-iii-2>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Red circle with red chord, yellow and blue radii to chord endpoints, solid+dotted black line from center through chord to circumference, and blue, black, and yellow angle sectors.
