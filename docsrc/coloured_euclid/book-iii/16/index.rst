Proposition III.16 — Perpendicular at Diameter Extremity Is Tangent
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XVI. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 96–97*)

The straight line :byrne-line:`yellow solid` drawn from the extremity of the diameter :byrne-line:`black solid` of a circle perpendicular to it falls without the circle. And if any straight line :byrne-line:`red dotted` be drawn from a point within that perpendicular to the point of contact, it cuts the circle.
