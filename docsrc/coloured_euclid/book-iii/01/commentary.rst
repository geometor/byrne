Commentary on Proposition III.1: Finding the Centre of a Circle
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0108 (p. 74) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.1 <byrne-iii-1>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue circle with bisected black diameter, solid red and dotted red chord perpendicular to it, blue and yellow right-angle sectors, and solid blue and dotted black construction lines.

**Logical Citations in Print:** ``B. 1, def. 15``, ``B. 1, pr. 8``, :just:`I.post.4`.
