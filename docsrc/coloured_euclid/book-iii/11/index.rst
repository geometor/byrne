Proposition III.11 — Line of Centres Passes Through Internal Contact
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XI. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 87–88*)

If two circles :byrne-circle:`blue` and :byrne-circle:`black` touch one another internally, the right line joining their centres, being produced, shall pass through a point of contact.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 121 (pp. 87–88)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Black outer circle and blue inner circle touching at top; black line joining centers extended dotted yellow and solid blue, forming a yellow-filled triangle with red and dotted blue radii to the contact point.
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** :just:`I.20`
