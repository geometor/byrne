Commentary on Proposition III.8: Secants and Tangents from an Exterior Point
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0116 (pp. 82–84) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.8 <byrne-iii-8>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Three black circles (one on 0116 for Part I, two on 0117 for Parts II and III) with exterior point above, vertical line through center, blue, red, and yellow secants/radii, and yellow, black, and blue angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.20`, :just:`I.24`, Axiom 5, :just:`I.21`, :just:`I.4`.
