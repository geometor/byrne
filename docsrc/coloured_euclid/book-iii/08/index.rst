Proposition III.8 — Secants and Tangents from an Exterior Point
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition VIII. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 82–84*)

I. If from a point without a circle, straight lines {(solid+dotted black line), :byrne-line:`red solid`, :byrne-line:`blue solid` &c.} are drawn to the circumference; of those falling upon the concave circumference the greatest is that ((solid+dotted black line)) which passes through the centre, and the line (:byrne-line:`red solid`) which is nearer the greatest is greater than that (:byrne-line:`blue solid`) which is more remote. II. Of those lines falling on the convex circumference the least is that (:byrne-line:`black dashed`) which being produced would pass through the centre, and the line which is nearer to the least is less than that which is more remote. III. Also the lines making equal angles with that which passes through the centre are equal, whether falling on the concave or convex circumference; and no third line can be drawn equal to them from the same point to the circumference.
