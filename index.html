the works of

Oliver Byrne¶

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of Oliver Byrne (1810–1880), the Irish civil engineer and mathematician celebrated for his revolutionary four-color visual edition of Euclid’s Elements.

The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid

Oliver Byrne’s landmark 1847 Chiswick Press edition using brilliant four-color diagrams and symbols in place of letters for Euclid’s Books I–VI.
