Book III Summary — Cyclic Angles & Power of a Point

Inscribed & Semicircular Angles (:ref:`III.20 <byrne-iii-20>`, :ref:`III.31 <byrne-iii-31>`): Combining isosceles radius triangles (I.5) with the exterior-angle theorem (I.32) proves that central angles double inscribed angles (III.20) and that every angle inscribed in a semicircle is a right angle (III.31).

Book IV Summary — From the Incircle to the Regular Pentagon

The Incenter & Equal Perpendicular Radii (:ref:`IV.4 <byrne-iv-4>`): Bisecting two interior angles (I.9) locates the incenter (Kimberling X(1) in geometor/etc), whose three dotted perpendiculars (I.12) are proved equal by AAS congruence (I.26) so the circle touches all three sides (III.16).

Book V Summary — Eudoxian Proportion as Typed Colored Algebra

Redundant Shape + Color Dual Coding: Byrne pairs distinct shapes (circle, square, diamond, shield, pentagon, triangle) with his four colors (red, yellow, blue, black), giving every term of a four- or six-magnitude proportion a unique silhouette even without color cues.

Book VI Summary — Unifying Spatial Form and Eudoxian Ratio

Proportionality Without Commensurability: Armed with Book V’s general ratio calculus, Book VI proves Thales’s intercept theorem (VI.2), AAA/SAS similarity (VI.4–VI.7), and quadratic area scaling (VI.19–VI.20) without ever assuming rational side ratios.
