Content of type 'book'

Book I — Congruence, Parallels & The Pythagorean Theorem

Book I ascends from elementary compass-and-straightedge construction (I.1) through triangle congruence (I.4, I.5), vertical angles (I.15), and parallel transport (I.32) to square construction (I.46) and Oliver Byrne's four-color Pythagorean climax (I.47). Each proposition below opens on its dedicated page with interactive inline color glyphs and exact geometor.model plates.

Book II — Geometric Algebra & The Golden Section

Definition I. Every right-angled parallelogram is said to be contained (×) by any two of the straight lines which contain one of the right angles.

Book III — Circles, Inscribed Angles & Tangent-Secant Power

Book III investigates the incidence and angular geometry of circles, culminating in three foundational theorems rendered with crystalline economy in Byrne’s four-color notation: central-to-inscribed angle doubling (III.20), Thales’s semicircle theorem (III.31), and the tangent-secant power of an external point (III.36).

Book IV — Inscribed Circles, The Golden Triangle & Regular Pentagon

Book IV unites the triangle congruence of Book I, the golden-section area algebra of Book II (II.11), and the tangent-chord circle theorems of Book III (III.36–III.37) to construct inscribed circles (IV.4), the $36^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ$ Golden Triangle (IV.10), and the regular pentagon (IV.11).

Book V — Color-Coded Symbolic Algebra of Eudoxian Proportions

In Book V, Eudoxus and Euclid generalize ratio and proportion from spatial lines to arbitrary continuous magnitudes of the same kind—lengths, planar areas, polyhedral volumes, angles, or time intervals. Book VI — Similar Figures & Extreme and Mean Ratio

Definition I. Similar rectilineal figures are those which have their several angles equal, each to each, and the sides about the equal angles proportionals (∷).
