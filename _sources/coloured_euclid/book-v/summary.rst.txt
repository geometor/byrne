Book V Summary: Eudoxian Proportion as Typed Colored Algebra
============================================================

.. rubric:: Why Book V Is Byrne's Most Modern Algebraic Innovation

.. folio:: 0179
   :page: 145

1. **Redundant Shape + Color Dual Coding**:
   Byrne pairs distinct **shapes** (*circle*, *square*, *diamond*, *shield*, *pentagon*, *triangle*) with his four **colors** (*red*, *yellow*, *blue*, *black*), giving every term of a four- or six-magnitude proportion a unique silhouette even without color cues.
2. **Elimination of False Metric Intuition**:
   Classical editions draw line segments of fixed lengths for Book V propositions, tempting learners to assume commensurability. Byrne's abstract tokens faithfully preserve Eudoxus's relational axioms across rational and irrational magnitudes (:math:`\sqrt{2}`, :math:`\phi`) alike.
3. **Verbal Compression Record (92.9% Reduction)**:
   Across :ref:`V.11 <byrne-v-11>`, :ref:`V.16 <byrne-v-16>`, :ref:`V.18 <byrne-v-18>`, and :ref:`V.22 <byrne-v-22>`, writing integer multipliers (:math:`M \cdot` :byrne-mag:`red shield`, :math:`m \cdot` :byrne-mag:`yellow square`) directly eliminates the explosion of auxiliary Greek/Latin letters (:math:`E, F, G, H, K, L`) needed for equimultiples in classical prose.
