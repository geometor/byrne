Book V Definitions I–IV (Part, Multiple & Ratio)
================================================

.. rubric:: Submultiples, Equimultiples & Homogeneous Magnitudes (*Pickering p. [145], Leaf 179*)

* **I.** A less magnitude is said to be an *aliquot part* or *submultiple* of a greater magnitude, when the less measures the greater; that is, when the less is contained a certain number of times exactly in the greater.
* **II.** A greater magnitude is said to be a *multiple* of a less, when the greater is measured by the less; that is, when the greater contains the less a certain number of times exactly.
* **III.** *Ratio* is a mutual relation of two magnitudes of the same kind to one another, with respect to quantity.
* **IV.** Magnitudes are said to have a *ratio to one another*, when the less can be multiplied so as to exceed the other (:just:`V.def.4`).
