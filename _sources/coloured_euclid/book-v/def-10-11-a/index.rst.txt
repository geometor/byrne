Book V Definitions X, XI & A — Duplicate, Triplicate & Compound Ratio
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Continued & Compounded Ratios (*Pickering pp. 200–202, Leaves 234–236*)

* **Definition X (p. 200):** When three magnitudes are continual proportionals, the first is said to have to the third the **duplicate ratio** of that which it has to the second.
* **Definition XI (pp. 200–201):** When four magnitudes are continual proportionals, the first has to the fourth the **triplicate ratio** of that which it has to the second.

.. folio:: 0235
   :page: 201

.. folio:: 0236
   :page: 202

* **Definition A (pp. 201–202):** When there are any number of magnitudes of the same kind, the ratio of the first to the last is said to be **compounded** of the ratios of the first to the second, the second to the third, and so on—introducing Simson's concluding propositions ``V.F``–``V.K``.
