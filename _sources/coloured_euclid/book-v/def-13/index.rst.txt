Book V Definition XIII — Permutando (Alternando)
================================================

.. rubric:: Alternate Proportion Nomenclature (*Pickering p. 180, Leaf 214*)

The technical term *permutando*, or *alternando* (by permutation or alternately), is used when there are four proportionals, and it is inferred that the first has the same ratio to the third which the second has to the fourth (:ref:`Prop. V.16 <byrne-v-16>`).
