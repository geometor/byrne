Book V Definitions XIX–XX — Ex Aequali
======================================

.. rubric:: Ordinate and Perturbate Ratio Equality (*Pickering pp. 188–189, Leaves 222–223*)

* **Definition XIX (p. 188):** *Ex æquali* (from equality of distance) used simply by itself, when the first magnitude is to the last of the first rank as the first is to the last of the second rank (:ref:`Prop. V.22 <byrne-v-22>`).

.. folio:: 0223
   :page: 189

* **Definition XX (p. 189):** *Ex æquali in proportione perturbatâ seu inordinatâ* (perturbate or disorderly proportion), when ratios cross-match in reverse order (:ref:`Prop. V.23 <byrne-v-23>`).
