Proposition V.11 — Transitivity of Ratios
=========================================

.. rubric:: Book V, Proposition XI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 175* · ``G.271``)

*Ratios that are the same to the same ratio, are the same to each other.*


.. picture:: /_static/figures/v11.svg
   :alt: Book V Proposition 11 — Chiswick Press colored magnitude tableau for transitivity of ratios.
   :caption: **Pickering p. 175 Tableau.** Transitivity of Eudoxian ratios across three colored magnitude pairs.

.. picture:: 0209.g1.png
   :alt: Oliver Byrne 1847 Pickering archival full-RGB plate for Proposition V.11 (Leaf 209).
   :caption: **1847 Chiswick Press Archival Plate (Leaf 209, p. 175).** Full-RGB reproduction of Byrne's four-color woodblock print.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Given.** Let :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`blue square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow shield` and :byrne-mag:`red circle` : :byrne-mag:`yellow shield` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`black pentagon` : :byrne-mag:`black circle`;

   then will :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`blue square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`black pentagon` : :byrne-mag:`black circle`.

   **Demonstration (:just:`V.def.5`).**

   For arbitrary positive integers :math:`M, m`:

   If :math:`M \cdot` :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` is :byrne-op:`gt`, ``=``, or :byrne-op:`lt` :math:`m \cdot` :byrne-mag:`blue square`,

   then :math:`M \cdot` :byrne-mag:`red circle` is correspondingly :byrne-op:`gt`, ``=``, or :byrne-op:`lt` :math:`m \cdot` :byrne-mag:`yellow shield` (:just:`V.def.5`),

   and therefore :math:`M \cdot` :byrne-mag:`black pentagon` is correspondingly :byrne-op:`gt`, ``=``, or :byrne-op:`lt` :math:`m \cdot` :byrne-mag:`black circle` (:just:`V.def.5`);

   :byrne-op:`therefore` if :math:`M \cdot` :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` is :byrne-op:`gt`, ``=``, or :byrne-op:`lt` :math:`m \cdot` :byrne-mag:`blue square`, then :math:`M \cdot` :byrne-mag:`black pentagon` is :byrne-op:`gt`, ``=``, or :byrne-op:`lt` :math:`m \cdot` :byrne-mag:`black circle`,

   and :byrne-op:`therefore` (:just:`V.def.5`) :byrne-mag:`blue diamond` : :byrne-mag:`blue square` :byrne-op:`prop` :byrne-mag:`black pentagon` : :byrne-mag:`black circle`. **Q.E.D.**
