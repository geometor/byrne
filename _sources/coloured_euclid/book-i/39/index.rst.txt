Proposition I.39 — Equal Triangles on the Same Base Share Parallels
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXXIX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 40*)

Equal triangles :byrne-tri:`yellow filled` and (yellow-and-black triangle) on the same base (:byrne-line:`black solid`) and on the same side of it, are between the same parallels.
