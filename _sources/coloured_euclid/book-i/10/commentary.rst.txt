Commentary on Proposition I.10: Bisecting a Finite Straight Line
================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0044 (p. 10) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.10 <byrne-i-10>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; equilateral triangle with yellow left side, blue right side, solid-black left half and dotted-black right half of base, and vertical red bisector dividing the top angle into blue and yellow.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.1`, :just:`I.9`, :just:`I.4`, *const*.
