Commentary on Proposition I.35: Parallelograms on the Same Base and Parallels
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0070 (p. 36) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.35 <byrne-i-35>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; two overlapping yellow-filled parallelograms on a black base with a blue overlapping base triangle and an unshaded outlined triangle at the upper right; red, blue, black, and white corner angles along the top.
