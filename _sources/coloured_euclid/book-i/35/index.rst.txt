Proposition I.35 — Parallelograms on the Same Base and Parallels
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXXV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 36*)

Parallelograms on the same base, and between the same parallels, are (in area) equal.

----------------------------------------
