Proposition I.22 — Constructing a Triangle from Three Given Lines
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXII. Problem (*Pickering p. 22*)

Given three right lines {:byrne-line:`black dotted`, :byrne-line:`red dotted`, :byrne-line:`blue dotted`} the sum of any two greater than the third, to construct a triangle whose sides shall be respectively equal to the given lines.
