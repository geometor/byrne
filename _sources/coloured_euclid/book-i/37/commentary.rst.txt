Commentary on Proposition I.37: Triangles on the Same Base and Parallels
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0072 (p. 38) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.37 <byrne-i-37>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; two overlapping triangles (yellow, and yellow-and-black) on a shared black base between dotted parallels, flanked by blue (left) and red (right) triangles completing two parallelograms.
