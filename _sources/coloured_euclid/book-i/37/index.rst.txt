Proposition I.37 — Triangles on the Same Base and Parallels
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXXVII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 38*)

Triangles :byrne-tri:`yellow filled` and (yellow-and-black triangle) on the same base (:byrne-line:`black solid`) and between the same parallels are equal.
