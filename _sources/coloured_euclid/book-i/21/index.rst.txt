Proposition I.21 — Interior Cevian Triangle Inequality and Greater Angle
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 21*)

If from any point ((black-and-yellow chevron)) within a triangle (triangle with blue, red/dotted-red, and dotted-black sides) straight lines be drawn to the extremities of one side ((dotted-black line)), these lines must be together less than the other two sides, but must contain a greater angle.
