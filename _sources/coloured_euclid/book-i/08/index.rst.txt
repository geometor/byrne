Proposition I.8 — Side-Side-Side Congruence
===========================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition VIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 8*)

If two triangles have two sides of the one respectively equal to two sides of the other (:byrne-line:`blue solid` = :byrne-line:`blue solid` and :byrne-line:`red solid` = :byrne-line:`red solid`), and also their bases (:byrne-line:`black solid` = :byrne-line:`black solid`), equal; then the angles (:byrne-angle:`black` and :byrne-angle:`black`) contained by their equal sides are also equal.
