Proposition I.26 — Angle-Side-Angle and Angle-Angle-Side Congruence
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXVI. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 26–27*)

If two triangles have two angles of the one respectively equal to two angles of the other, (:byrne-angle:`yellow` = :byrne-angle:`yellow` and :byrne-angle:`red` = :byrne-angle:`black`), and a side of the one equal to a side of the other similarly placed with respect to the equal angles, the remaining sides and angles are respectively equal to one another.
