Archival Note: The 1847 Title-Page Transposition of ``I.47``
------------------------------------------------------------

Direct multimodal inspection of the 1847 Pickering volume reveals a telling bibliographic detail between **Leaf 7 (Title Page)** and **Leaves 82–83 (pp. 48–49)**:

* On **pp. 48–49** (the proof of :ref:`I.47 <byrne-i-47>`), the narrow left hypotenuse rectangle under the short leg is printed in **ochre yellow** (:byrne-rect:`yellow filled`, equal to the **black** leg square :byrne-sq:`black filled`) and the wide right hypotenuse rectangle under the long leg is printed in **Prussian blue** (:byrne-rect:`blue filled`, equal to the **red** leg square :byrne-sq:`red filled`).
* On the **1847 Title Page emblem** (Leaf 7), :person:`Whittingham's <whittingham>` pressman transposed the yellow and blue woodblocks on the bottom square, coloring the narrow left strip blue and the wide right rectangle yellow. Our symbolic ``i47.svg`` plate faithfully follows the mathematically canonical proof plate on **pp. 48–49**.

Chromatic Register Allocation Across Book I
-------------------------------------------

Across Book I's seven showcase plates, Byrne demonstrates every core idiom of his visual language:

* **Direct radius sharing** in :ref:`I.1 <byrne-i-1>`, where two intersecting colored circles turn three segment equalities into an immediate visual transitivity chain.
* **Congruence-twin pairing** in :ref:`I.4 <byrne-i-4>` and :ref:`I.5 <byrne-i-5>`, assigning identical ink colors to symmetric segments and angles so ``SAS`` superposition becomes self-evident.
* **Additive sector equations** in :ref:`I.15 <byrne-i-15>` and :ref:`I.32 <byrne-i-32>`, treating colored angle wedges as algebraic summands equal to :byrne-op:`two-right-angles`.
* **Boundary stroke modulation** in :ref:`I.47 <byrne-i-47>`, using :byrne-line:`blue dotted` and :byrne-line:`red dashed` edges to cite individual sides of solid colored polygons without adding a fifth ink.
