Commentary on Proposition I.28: Exterior or Supplementary Interior Angles Imply Parallels
=========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0063 (p. 29) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.28 <byrne-i-28>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper right; two horizontal parallel lines (red top, yellow bottom) cut by a slanted black line, forming a four-sector colored disk (black, yellow, blue, red) above and a two-sector semicircle (blue, red) below.
