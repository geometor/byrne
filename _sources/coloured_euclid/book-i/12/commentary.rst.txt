Commentary on Proposition I.12: Perpendicular from an External Point
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0046 (p. 12) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.12 <byrne-i-12>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; triangle with blue equal sides, vertical red perpendicular to a horizontal black-and-yellow base (with yellow and blue quarter-circle angles), and a red circular arc below with dotted red extensions.
