Proposition I.16 — Exterior Angle Exceeds Either Remote Interior
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XVI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 16*)

If a side of a triangle ((triangle with dotted yellow, dotted blue, and black sides)) is produced, the external angle ((black-and-white sector with dotted base)) is greater than either of the internal remote angles (:byrne-angle:`black` or :byrne-angle:`blue`).
