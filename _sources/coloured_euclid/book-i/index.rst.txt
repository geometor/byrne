Book I — Congruence, Parallels & The Pythagorean Theorem
========================================================

.. rubric:: Showcase Propositions of Book I (*Pickering pp. 1–50, Leaves 35–84*)

Book I ascends from elementary compass-and-straightedge construction (:ref:`I.1 <byrne-i-1>`) through triangle congruence (:ref:`I.4 <byrne-i-4>`, :ref:`I.5 <byrne-i-5>`), vertical angles (:ref:`I.15 <byrne-i-15>`), and parallel transport (:ref:`I.32 <byrne-i-32>`) to square construction (:ref:`I.46 <byrne-i-46>`) and :person:`Oliver Byrne's <byrne>` four-color Pythagorean climax (:ref:`I.47 <byrne-i-47>`). Each proposition below opens on its dedicated page with interactive inline color glyphs and exact ``geometor.model`` plates.
