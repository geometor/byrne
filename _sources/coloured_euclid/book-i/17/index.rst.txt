Proposition I.17 — Sum of Two Angles Less Than Two Right Angles
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XVII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 17*)

Any two angles of a triangle (triangle with red, blue, and black sides) are together less than two right angles.
