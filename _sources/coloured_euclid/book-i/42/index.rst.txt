Proposition I.42 — Parallelogram Equal to a Given Triangle
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XLII. Problem (*Pickering p. 43*)

To construct a parallelogram equal to a given triangle (yellow-blue-black triangle) and having an angle equal to a given rectilinear angle :byrne-angle:`yellow`.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 77 (p. 43)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Upper right; yellow parallelogram (dotted red left edge, blue top, red right edge) overlapping a triangle subdivided into blue and black halves along a solid/dotted black base, with a separate yellow angle at upper right. (Running header misprints THEOR. for PROB.)
