Commentary on Proposition I.48: Converse of the Pythagorean Theorem
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0084 (p. 50) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.48 <byrne-i-48>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; two right triangles back-to-back along a shared vertical blue side—dotted red hypotenuse and dotted black base on the left with a yellow right-angle sector, and solid red hypotenuse and solid black base on the right with a red right-angle sector.
