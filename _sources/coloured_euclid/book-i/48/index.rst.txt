Proposition I.48 — Converse of the Pythagorean Theorem
======================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XLVIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 50*)

If the square of one side (:byrne-line:`red solid`) of a triangle is equal to the squares of the other two sides (:byrne-line:`blue solid` and :byrne-line:`black solid`), the angle (:byrne-angle:`yellow`) subtended by that side is a right angle.
