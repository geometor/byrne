Commentary on Proposition I.44: Applying a Parallelogram Equal to a Triangle
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0079 (p. 45) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.44 <byrne-i-44>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper right; parallelogram complement construction containing yellow (upper) and blue (lower) parallelograms along a red diagonal with blue, red, and black angle sectors, accompanied by a red triangle (upper left) and yellow angle (lower right).

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.42`, :just:`I.43`, *const*, ``pr. 19. and const``.
