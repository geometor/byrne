Commentary on Proposition I.18: Greater Side Subtends Greater Angle
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0052 (p. 18) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.18 <byrne-i-18>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left; triangle with solid/dotted red left side, blue right side, black base, and yellow interior segment from bottom-right to left side; yellow, blue, and black-red angles.
