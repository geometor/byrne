Proposition I.6 — Converse of Isosceles Base Angles
===================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition VI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 6*)

In any triangle ((triangle with solid-and-dotted black, blue, and red sides)) if two angles (:byrne-angle:`yellow` and :byrne-angle:`black`) are equal, the sides (:byrne-line:`black solid-dotted` and :byrne-line:`blue solid`) opposite to them are also equal.
