Proposition I.14 — Converse of Straight-Angle Sum
=================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XIV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 14*)

If two straight lines (:byrne-line:`blue solid` and :byrne-line:`black solid`), meeting a third straight line (:byrne-line:`red solid`), at the same point, and at opposite sides of it, make with it adjacent angles (:byrne-angle:`yellow` and :byrne-angle:`blue`) equal to two right angles; these straight lines lie in one continuous straight line.
