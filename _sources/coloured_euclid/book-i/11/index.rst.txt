Proposition I.11 — Perpendicular from a Point on a Line
=======================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XI. Problem (*Pickering p. 11*)

From a given point ((black-and-red line)), in a given straight line ((black-and-red line)), to draw a perpendicular.
