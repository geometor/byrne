Proposition I.19 — Greater Angle Subtended by Greater Side
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XIX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 19*)

If in any triangle (triangle with red, blue, and black sides) one angle :byrne-angle:`blue` be greater than another :byrne-angle:`red` the side :byrne-line:`blue solid` which is opposite to the greater angle, is greater than the side :byrne-line:`red solid` opposite the less.
