Proposition I.30 — Transitivity of Parallelism
==============================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 31*)

Straight lines ((red and blue lines)) which are parallel to the same straight line (:byrne-line:`yellow solid`), are parallel to one another.
