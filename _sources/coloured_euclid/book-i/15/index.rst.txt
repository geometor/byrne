Proposition I.15 — Equality of Vertical Angles
==============================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 15* · ``G.104``)

*If two right lines* (:byrne-line:`black solid <i15:/ A B />` *and* :byrne-line:`red solid <i15:/ C D />`) *intersect one another, the vertical angles* :byrne-angle:`yellow <i15:< C E A )>` *and* :byrne-angle:`black <i15:< D E B )>`, :byrne-angle:`red <i15:< B E C )>` *and* :byrne-angle:`blue <i15:< A E D )>` *are equal.*

.. picture:: /_static/figures/i15.svg
   :alt: Book I Proposition 15 — Intersecting black and red straight lines forming red, yellow, blue, and black vertical angle sectors.
   :caption: **Book I, Prop. XV (p. 15 · G.104).** Adjacent supplementary pairs cancel common sectors to prove vertical angle equality.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Demonstration (:just:`I.13`, :just:`C.N.1`, :just:`C.N.3`).**

   :byrne-angle:`yellow <i15:< C E A )>` + :byrne-angle:`red <i15:< B E C )>` = :byrne-op:`two-right-angles` (:just:`I.13`),

   :byrne-angle:`black <i15:< D E B )>` + :byrne-angle:`red <i15:< B E C )>` = :byrne-op:`two-right-angles` (:just:`I.13`),

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-angle:`yellow <i15:< C E A )>` = :byrne-angle:`black <i15:< D E B )>` (:just:`C.N.1`, :just:`C.N.3`).

   In the same manner it may be shown that

   :byrne-angle:`red <i15:< B E C )>` = :byrne-angle:`blue <i15:< A E D )>`. **Q.E.D.**
