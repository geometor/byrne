Book I Summary: Deductive Arc (I.1 → I.47)
==========================================

.. rubric:: From Equilateral Construction to Pythagorean Area Decomposition

Book I ascends in three architectural stages, each illuminated by :person:`Oliver Byrne's <byrne>` four-color register design:

1. **Pure Incidence & Triangle Congruence (:ref:`I.1 <byrne-i-1>`–``I.26``)**:
   Without parallel lines, :person:`Euclid <euclid>` constructs the equilateral triangle (:ref:`I.1 <byrne-i-1>`), establishes SAS congruence (:ref:`I.4 <byrne-i-4>`), proves isosceles base-angle equality in the *Pons Asinorum* (:ref:`I.5 <byrne-i-5>`), bisects angles and segments (``I.9``–``I.10``), erects perpendiculars (``I.11``–``I.12``), and proves vertical-angle equality (:ref:`I.15 <byrne-i-15>`).
2. **Parallel Transport & Angle Sum (``I.27``–``I.34``)**:
   Crossing the threshold of the Parallel Postulate (Simson ``Axiom XII`` / :just:`I.post.5`) in ``I.29``, Euclid constructs parallels (``I.31``) and proves in :ref:`I.32 <byrne-i-32>` that the exterior angle of any triangle equals the sum of its two opposite interior angles—displaying the conservation of :byrne-op:`two-right-angles` visually as adjacent colored wedges.
3. **Parallelogram Shear & The Pythagorean Climax (``I.35``–``I.48``)**:
   Area equivalence under shear between parallel lines (``I.35``–``I.41``) and square construction (:ref:`I.46 <byrne-i-46>`) culminate in :ref:`I.47 <byrne-i-47>`, where the altitude from the right angle splits the hypotenuse square into two colored rectangles individually equal to the squares on the adjacent legs.
