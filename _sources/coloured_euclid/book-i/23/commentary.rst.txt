Commentary on Proposition I.23: Copying a Rectilineal Angle
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0057 (p. 23) · Book I Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition I.23 <byrne-i-23>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Right side; two stacked triangles, upper with blue/dotted-blue side, black/dotted-black base, red closing side, and red angle; lower with yellow side, black/dotted-black base, red closing side, and blue angle.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.22`, :just:`I.8`.
