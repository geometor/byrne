Proposition I.27 — Equal Alternate Angles Imply Parallels
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XXVII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 28*)

If a straight line (:byrne-line:`black solid`) meeting two other straight lines, (:byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`blue solid`) makes with them the alternate angles (:byrne-angle:`blue` and :byrne-angle:`red`; :byrne-angle:`yellow` and :byrne-angle:`yellow`) equal, these two straight lines are parallel.
