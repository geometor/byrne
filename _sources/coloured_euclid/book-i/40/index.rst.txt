Proposition I.40 — Equal Triangles on Equal Bases Share Parallels
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XL. Theorem (*Pickering p. 41*)

Equal triangles (:byrne-tri:`yellow filled` and :byrne-tri:`red filled`) on equal bases, and on the same side, are between the same parallels.

----------------------------------------
