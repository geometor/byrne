Proposition I.41 — Parallelogram Double a Triangle on Same Base
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book I, Proposition XLI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 42*)

If a parallelogram (yellow-and-blue parallelogram) and a triangle (blue-yellow-red triangle) are upon the same base :byrne-line:`black solid` and between the same parallels :byrne-line:`black dotted` and :byrne-line:`black solid`, the parallelogram is double the triangle.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 76 (p. 42)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Upper left; parallelogram (split into blue and yellow triangles) sharing a black base and dotted top parallel with a triangle partitioned into blue, yellow, and red filled regions.
