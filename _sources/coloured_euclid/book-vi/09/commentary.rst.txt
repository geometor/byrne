Commentary on Proposition VI.9: Cutting Off Any Required Submultiple Part of a Line
===================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0261 (p. 227) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.9 <byrne-vi-9>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A triangle formed by a solid-and-dotted yellow line, a solid-and-dotted blue vertical line (produced upward as dotted black), and a solid red top line, with a parallel dotted red line cutting off the solid yellow segment.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`VI.2`, :just:`V.18`.
