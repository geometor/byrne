Proposition VI.21 — Similarity to the Same Figure Is Transitive
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 246*)

RECTILINEAR figures (:byrne-tri:`red filled` and :byrne-tri:`blue filled`) which are similar to the same figure (:byrne-tri:`yellow filled`) are similar also to each other.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 280 (p. 246)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Three similar solid right-angled triangles stacked vertically on the left: large red (top), medium blue (middle), and small yellow (bottom).
