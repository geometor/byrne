Book VI — Similar Figures & Extreme and Mean Ratio
==================================================

.. rubric:: Proportional Geometry & Application of Areas (*Pickering pp. 213–268, Leaves 247–302*)

Book VI reunites the abstract Eudoxian proportion calculus of :ref:`Book V <byrne-book-v>` with planar figures—developing similarity, parallel proportion, mean proportionals, and the proportional division of a line into extreme and mean ratio (:ref:`VI.30 <byrne-vi-30>`).

   :alt: Book VI Featured Plate — Cutting a finite straight line in extreme and mean ratio by application of areas (Proposition VI.30).
   :caption: **Book VI Climax — Proposition VI.30 (p. 258 · G.328).** Proportional counterpart to :ref:`II.11 <byrne-ii-11>`, cutting a line in extreme and mean ratio ($1 : \phi$) via similar rectangles.

Book VI Key Definitions & Deductive Arc (pp. 213–259)
-----------------------------------------------------

* **Definition I.** *Similar rectilineal figures* are those which have their several angles equal, each to each, and the sides about the equal angles proportionals (:byrne-op:`prop`).
* **Definition III.** A straight line is said to be *cut in extreme and mean ratio* (:term:`Golden Section <extreme-and-mean-ratio>`) when the whole line is to the greater segment as the greater segment is to the less.
* **Props. VI.1–VI.13 (``G.286``–``G.298``):** Proportional triangles, parallel intercept theorem, angle bisector ratio, and semicircular geometric mean $\sqrt{ab}$.
* **Prop. VI.31 (p. 259 · ``G.329``):** Generalized Pythagorean Theorem extending :ref:`I.47 <byrne-i-47>` to *any three similar and similarly described rectilineal figures*.
