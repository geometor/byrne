Commentary on Proposition VI.27: Greatest Parallelogram Applied Deficient by a Similar Figure
=============================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0288 (p. 254) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.27 <byrne-vi-27>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A red square on the left half of the yellow-red-blue baseline, overlapping a horizontal rectangle made of a dark blue square/rectangle on the left and a yellow rectangle extending to the right.
