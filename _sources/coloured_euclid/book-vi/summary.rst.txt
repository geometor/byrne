Book VI Summary: Unifying Spatial Form and Eudoxian Ratio
=========================================================

.. rubric:: How Book VI Reconnects Book V Algebra to Planar Geometry

1. **Proportionality Without Commensurability**:
   Armed with Book V's general ratio calculus, Book VI proves Thales's intercept theorem (``VI.2``), AAA/SAS similarity (``VI.4``–``VI.7``), and quadratic area scaling (``VI.19``–``VI.20``) without ever assuming rational side ratios.
2. **Two Faces of the Golden Ratio (:ref:`II.11 <byrne-ii-11>` vs. :ref:`VI.30 <byrne-vi-30>`)**:
   Whereas :ref:`II.11 <byrne-ii-11>` formulated the golden cut purely as an *area equality* ($AH^2 = AB \cdot HB$) prior to Book V, :ref:`VI.30 <byrne-vi-30>` expresses it directly as a *continuous proportion* ($AB : AE \mathrel{∷} AE : EB$) via ``VI.14`` and hyperbolic application of areas (``VI.29``).
