Commentary on Proposition VI.11: Third Proportional to Two Straight Lines
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0263 (p. 229) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.11 <byrne-vi-11>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two rays meeting at the bottom vertex: a solid black segment extended by a dotted blue segment, and a dotted red segment extended by a solid red segment, joined by a solid yellow line and a parallel dotted yellow line; a separate blue reference segment stands at right.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.31`, :just:`VI.2`, :just:`V.7`.
