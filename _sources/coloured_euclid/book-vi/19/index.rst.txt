Proposition VI.19 — Ratio of Similar Triangles Is Duplicate Ratio of Sides
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XIX. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 241–242*)

SIMILAR triangles (:byrne-tri:`yellow filled` and (red-and-blue triangle)) are to one another in the duplicate ratio of their homologous sides.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 275 (pp. 241–242)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** A smaller solid yellow triangle (blue, red, black sides, black apex angle) beside a larger triangle divided by a dotted yellow transversal into a lower red triangle and an upper blue triangle (with red apex angle and dotted-and-solid black left side).
