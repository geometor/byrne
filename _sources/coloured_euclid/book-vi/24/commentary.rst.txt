Commentary on Proposition VI.24: Parallelograms About a Diagonal Are Similar to the Whole
=========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0284 (p. 250) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.24 <byrne-vi-24>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A large parallelogram divided by lines through a point on its diagonal into four sub-parallelograms: the lower-left and upper-right about the diagonal, with blue, yellow, and red filled triangles along the upper-left side of the diagonal.
