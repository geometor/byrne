Proposition VI.7 — SSA Similarity with Equal Remaining Angles
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition VII. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 224–225*)

IF two triangles ((solid triangle: black, red, yellow) and (dotted triangle: blue, red, yellow)) have one angle in each equal ((black-outlined yellow angle) equal to (solid blue angle)), the sides about two other angles proportional (:byrne-line:`red solid` : :byrne-line:`yellow solid` :: :byrne-line:`red dotted` : :byrne-line:`yellow dotted`), and each of the remaining angles ((solid red angle) and (black-outlined red angle)) either less or not less than a right angle, the triangles are equiangular, and those angles are equal about which the sides are proportional.
