Proposition VI.1 — Triangles and Parallelograms of Equal Altitude Are as Bases
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition I. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 212–213*)

TRIANGLES and parallelograms having the same altitude are to one another as their bases.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 246 (pp. 212–213)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** A triangular fan sharing a common apex and collinear base, made of five alternating black-and-white triangles on the left, a solid red and a solid blue triangle in the middle (resting on blue and red base segments), and three alternating yellow-and-white triangles on the right.
