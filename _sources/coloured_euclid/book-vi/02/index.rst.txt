Proposition VI.2 — Parallel Line Divides Triangle Sides Proportionally
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition II. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 214–215*)

IF a straight line :byrne-line:`black solid` be drawn parallel to any side :byrne-line:`black dotted` of a triangle, it shall cut the other sides, or those sides produced, into proportional segments. And if any straight line :byrne-line:`black solid` divide the sides of a triangle, or those sides produced, into proportional segments, it is parallel to the remaining side :byrne-line:`black dotted`.
