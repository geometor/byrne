Commentary on Proposition VI.2: Parallel Line Divides Triangle Sides Proportionally
===================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0248 (pp. 214–215) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.2 <byrne-vi-2>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Three triangle configurations illustrating the parallel transversal (solid black line) and dotted black base with crossing red and blue diagonals and colored side segments (yellow, dotted red, dotted yellow, dotted blue): left (transversal across sides produced beyond the vertex), top-center (transversal cutting interior of sides), and bottom (transversal across sides produced below the base).
