Proposition VI.32 — Angle-Linked Proportional Sides Align into Collinearity
===========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXXII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 260*)

IF two triangles ((triangle with blue, red, yellow sides) and (triangle with dotted black, dotted red, dotted yellow sides)), have two sides proportional (:byrne-line:`blue solid` : :byrne-line:`red solid` :: :byrne-line:`black dotted` : :byrne-line:`red dotted`), and be so placed at an angle that the homologous sides are parallel, the remaining sides (:byrne-line:`yellow solid` and :byrne-line:`yellow dotted`) form one right line.
