Book VI Notes: Application of Areas & Projective Cross-Repository Links
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Historical & Symbolic Commentary on Book VI

Hyperbolic Application of Areas (``VI.29`` → ``VI.30``)
-------------------------------------------------------

As :person:`Proclus <proclus>` (:cite:`Friedlein <proclus:friedlein>`, ``geometor/proclus``) and :person:`Heath <heath>` (:cite:`1908 <heath:1908>`) explain, Greek *application of areas* (*parabole*, *elleipsis*, *hyperbole*) is the direct geometric ancestor of :person:`Apollonius's <euclid>` conic sections (``geometor/apollonius``). In :ref:`VI.30 <byrne-vi-30>`, applying a rectangle of area $1$ to a unit segment with square *excess* ($\phi^{-1}(1 + \phi^{-1}) = 1$) solves $x(1 + x) = 1 \implies x = \phi^{-1} = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$ in $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$.

Connections to ``geometor/pappus`` & ``geometor/divine``
--------------------------------------------------------

Book VI's intercept and angle-bisector proportions (``VI.2``–``VI.3``) form the metric gateway to projective cross-ratios, harmonic conjugates, and golden-section chains explored in ``geometor/pappus`` and ``geometor/divine``.
