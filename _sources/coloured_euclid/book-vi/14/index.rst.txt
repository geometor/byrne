Proposition VI.14 — Reciprocal Sides of Equal Equiangular Parallelograms
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XIV. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 232–233*)

I. EQUAL parallelograms :byrne-rect:`blue filled` and :byrne-rect:`yellow filled`, which have one angle in each equal, have the sides about the equal angles reciprocally proportional (:byrne-line:`red solid` : :byrne-line:`black solid` :: :byrne-line:`yellow solid` : :byrne-line:`blue solid`). II. And parallelograms which have one angle in each equal, and the sides about them reciprocally proportional, are equal.
