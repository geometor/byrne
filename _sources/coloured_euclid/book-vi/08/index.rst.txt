Proposition VI.8 — Altitude in Right Triangle Yields Similar Subtriangles
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition VIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 226*)

IN a right angled triangle ((yellow-and-red triangle)), if a perpendicular ((solid black vertical line)) be drawn from the right angle to the opposite side, the triangles (:byrne-tri:`yellow filled`, :byrne-tri:`red filled`) on each side of it are similar to the whole triangle and to each other.
