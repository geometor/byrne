Commentary on Proposition VI.18: Constructing a Similar Rectilineal Figure on a Given Line
==========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0273 (pp. 239–240) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.18 <byrne-vi-18>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two similar pentagons (larger above, smaller below) each decomposed into yellow, blue, and red triangles by diagonals (dotted red and dotted yellow in the upper, solid black/yellow in the lower), with black, red, and yellow angle arcs. (Note: Byrne's running head misprints 'THEOR.' instead of 'PROB.'; folio 0274 also prints 'B. 6. pr. 22' instead of 'B. 5. pr. 22'.)

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.32`, :just:`VI.4`, :just:`VI.22`, :just:`VI.def.1`.
