Book VI Definitions (I–IV)
==========================

.. rubric:: Similarity, Reciprocity, Extreme & Mean Ratio and Altitude (*Pickering p. [211], Leaf 245*)

.. picture:: 0245.g1.png
   :alt: Book VI Definitions solid blue similar triangles with golden and red comparison lines (Leaf 245, p. [211]).
   :caption: **Book VI Definitions Archival Plate (Leaf 245, p. [211]).** Equiangular similar figures rendered in Chiswick Press Prussian blue.

.. picture:: 0245.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram for Book VI Definition I (Similar Figures).
   :caption: **Calibrated Vector Diagram (0245.g1).** Equiangular triangles with proportional base segments.

* **Definition I:** *Similar rectilineal figures* are those which have their several angles equal, each to each, and the sides about the equal angles proportionals (:byrne-op:`prop`).
* **Definition II:** Two figures are *reciprocally proportional* when each has antecedent and consequent sides reversing between the two figures.
* **Definition III:** A straight line is said to be cut in *extreme and mean ratio* (:term:`Golden Section <extreme-and-mean-ratio>`) when the whole line is to the greater segment as the greater segment is to the less.
* **Definition IV:** The *altitude* of any figure is the straight line drawn from its vertex perpendicular (:byrne-op:`perp`) to the base.
