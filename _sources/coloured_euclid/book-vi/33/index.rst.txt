Proposition VI.33 — Angles at Centre and Circumference Are as Their Arcs
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXXIII. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 261–262*)

IN equal circles (:byrne-circle:`red`, :byrne-circle:`blue`), angles, whether at the centre or circumference, are in the same ratio to one another as the arcs on which they stand ((solid black central angle) : (black-outlined central angle) :: (solid black arc) : (dotted yellow arc)); so also are sectors.
