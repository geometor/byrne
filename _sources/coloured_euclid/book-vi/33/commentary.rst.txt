Commentary on Proposition VI.33: Angles at Centre and Circumference Are as Their Arcs
=====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0295 (pp. 261–262) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.33 <byrne-vi-33>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two equal circles (red circle above, blue circle below), each with radii dividing adjacent bottom arcs (black, red, blue above; dotted yellow, red, blue below) and showing colored central and inscribed circumferential angle sectors.
