Proposition VI.22 — Proportionality of Similar Figures on Four Proportional Lines
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXII. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 247–248*)

PART I. IF four straight lines be proportional (:byrne-line:`black solid` : :byrne-line:`blue solid` :: :byrne-line:`red solid` : :byrne-line:`yellow solid`), the similar rectilinear figures similarly described on them are also proportional. PART II. And if four similar rectilinear figures, similarly described on four straight lines, be proportional, the straight lines are also proportional.
