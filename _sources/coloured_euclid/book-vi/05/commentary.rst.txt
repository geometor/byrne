Commentary on Proposition VI.5: Triangles with Proportional Sides Are Equiangular
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0254 (pp. 220–221) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.5 <byrne-vi-5>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Left: a triangle (red, blue, black sides) sharing its black base with a downward constructed triangle (yellow and dotted yellow sides), with black, yellow, blue, and red angle sectors. Right: a separate smaller dotted triangle (red, blue, black sides) with blue, yellow, and red angle sectors.
