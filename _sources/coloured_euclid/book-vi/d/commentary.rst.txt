Commentary on Proposition VI.D: Ptolemy's Theorem (Cyclic Quadrilateral Diagonals)
==================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0301 (pp. 267–268) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.D <byrne-vi-d>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A quadrilateral (dotted black, yellow, solid black, dotted blue sides) inscribed in a red circle, with a solid-and-dotted red diagonal, a solid blue diagonal, and a dotted yellow auxiliary line from the top-left vertex, marked with blue, black, red, and yellow angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.23`, :just:`III.21`, :just:`VI.4`, :just:`VI.16`, :just:`II.1`.
