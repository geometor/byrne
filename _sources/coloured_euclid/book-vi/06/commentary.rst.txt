Commentary on Proposition VI.6: SAS Similarity of Triangles
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0256 (pp. 222–223) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.6 <byrne-vi-6>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Left: a triangle (red, blue, black sides) sharing a horizontal black base with a downward constructed triangle (yellow and dotted yellow sides), with yellow, blue, red, and black angle sectors. Right: a separate smaller dotted triangle (red, blue, black sides) with yellow, blue, and red angle sectors.
