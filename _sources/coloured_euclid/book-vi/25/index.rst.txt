Proposition VI.25 — Figure Similar to One and Equal to Another
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXV. Problem (*Pickering pp. 251–252*)

TO describe a rectilinear figure, which shall be similar to a given rectilinear figure (:byrne-tri:`red filled`), and equal to another (:byrne-mag:`blue pentagon`).

----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 285 (pp. 251–252)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Above: a yellow triangle (result) and a blue pentagon (given area). Below: the given red triangle resting on a blue parallelogram, adjoined on the right by an outlined white parallelogram with a dotted black top side and red and yellow corner angle sectors.
