Proposition VI.28 — Application of Areas Deficient by a Similar Parallelogram
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXVIII. Problem (*Pickering pp. 255–256*)

TO divide a given straight line ((dotted red - solid blue - dotted black line)) so that the rectangle contained by its segments may be equal to a given area, not exceeding the square of half the line.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 289 (pp. 255–256)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** A horizontal segmented line (dotted red, solid blue, dotted black) with a vertical red perpendicular at the midpoint, a yellow hypotenuse to the left intersection, and a red circular arc cutting the line below.
