Commentary on Proposition VI.4: Equiangular Triangles Have Proportional Sides
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0252 (pp. 218–219) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.4 <byrne-vi-4>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two equiangular triangles (solid blue/red/black left, dotted blue/red/black right) placed on a collinear base, with dotted yellow and solid yellow upper extensions meeting at the apex to form a parallelogram; equal angles are marked with yellow, blue, red, and black sectors and black and red arcs.
