Proposition VI.26 — Similar Parallelograms Sharing an Angle Share the Diagonal
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Book VI, Proposition XXVI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 253*)

IF similar and similarly posited parallelograms ((white parallelogram with red band) and (white parallelogram with blue band and diagonal)) have a common angle, they are about the same diagonal.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 287 (p. 253)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Coaxial nested parallelograms sharing a yellow bottom-left angle: an inner white parallelogram, a middle red band, and an outer blue band, with the true diagonal and a slightly curved hypothetical black diagonal.
