Commentary on Proposition VI.26: Similar Parallelograms Sharing an Angle Share the Diagonal
===========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0287 (p. 253) · Book VI Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition VI.26 <byrne-vi-26>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Coaxial nested parallelograms sharing a yellow bottom-left angle: an inner white parallelogram, a middle red band, and an outer blue band, with the true diagonal and a slightly curved hypothetical black diagonal.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.31`, :just:`VI.24`, :just:`V.9`.
