Proposition III.7 — Segments from a Non-Central Interior Point
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition VII. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 80–81*)

If from any point within a circle :byrne-circle:`blue` which is not the centre, lines {:byrne-line:`black dotted`, :byrne-line:`red solid`, :byrne-line:`blue solid`} are drawn to the circumference; the greatest of those lines is that ((solid+dotted black line)) which passes through the centre, and the least is the remaining part (:byrne-line:`yellow solid`) of the diameter. Of the others, that (:byrne-line:`red solid`) which is nearer to the line passing through the centre, is greater than that (:byrne-line:`blue solid`) which is more remote. Fig. 2. The two lines ((solid+dotted blue line) and :byrne-line:`red solid`) which make equal angles with that passing through the centre, on opposite sides of it, are equal to each other; and there cannot be drawn a third line equal to them, from the same point to the circumference.
