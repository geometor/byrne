Commentary on Proposition III.7: Segments from a Non-Central Interior Point
===========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0114 (pp. 80–81) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.7 <byrne-iii-7>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two blue circles (Fig. I and Fig. II) with vertical diameter through an interior off-center point (dotted black, solid black, yellow), red and blue lines to circumference, dotted radii, and yellow, black, and red angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.def.15`, :just:`I.20`, ``ax``, :just:`I.24`, :just:`I.4`.
