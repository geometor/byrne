Commentary on Proposition III.21: Angles in the Same Segment Are Equal
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0138 (p. 104) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.21 <byrne-iii-21>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Fig. I: blue circle, segment greater than semicircle, dotted black base, red and blue inscribed angles, yellow central angle; Fig. II: red circle, segment less than semicircle, dotted black base, blue diameter, red chord, yellow/red and blue/black inscribed angles, and blue and yellow bottom segments.
