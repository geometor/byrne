Commentary on Proposition III.37: Converse of Tangent-Secant Power
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0156 (p. 122) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.37 <byrne-iii-37>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Black circle with exterior point at upper right, secant (solid black inside, dotted black outside), red line to top, blue tangent to bottom, yellow line to center, dotted red and dotted blue radii, and blue and red angle sectors.
