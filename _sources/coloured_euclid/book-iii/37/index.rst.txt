Proposition III.37 — Converse of Tangent-Secant Power
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXXVII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 122*)

If from a point outside of a circle two straight lines be drawn, the one (solid black + dotted black line) cutting the circle, the other :byrne-line:`red solid` meeting it, and if the rectangle contained by the whole cutting line (solid black + dotted black line) and its external segment :byrne-line:`black dotted` be equal to the square of the line meeting the circle, the latter :byrne-line:`red solid` is a tangent to the circle.
