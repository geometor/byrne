Proposition III.28 — Equal Chords Cut Off Equal Arcs
====================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXVIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 112*)

In equal circles :byrne-circle:`yellow` and :byrne-circle:`black`, equal chords :byrne-line:`red solid`, :byrne-line:`red dotted` cut off equal arches.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 146 (p. 112)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Two equal circles: top yellow circle with blue lower arc, solid red chord, black and blue radii, and red central angle; bottom black circle with red lower arc, dotted red chord, dotted black and dotted blue radii, and yellow central angle.
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** :just:`III.26`, :just:`C.N.3`
