Commentary on Proposition III.26: Equal Angles Stand on Equal Arcs
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0143 (pp. 109–110) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.26 <byrne-iii-26>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two equal circles (top blue, bottom red) each with an inscribed angle (red in top, blue in bottom), a central angle (yellow in top, black in bottom), and a yellow-filled base segment.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.4`, :just:`III.20`, :just:`III.def.10`, :just:`III.24`, :just:`C.N.3`.
