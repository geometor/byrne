Proposition III.26 — Equal Angles Stand on Equal Arcs
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXVI. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 109–110*)

In equal circles :byrne-circle:`blue` and :byrne-circle:`red`, the arcs :byrne-arc:`black`, :byrne-arc:`black` on which stand equal angles, whether at the centre or circumference, are equal.

----------------------------------------
