Proposition III.18 — Radius to Point of Contact Is Perpendicular
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XVIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 100*)

If a right line :byrne-line:`blue dotted` be a tangent to a circle, the straight line :byrne-line:`blue solid` drawn from the centre to the point of contact, is perpendicular to it.
