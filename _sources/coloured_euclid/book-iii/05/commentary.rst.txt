Commentary on Proposition III.5: Intersecting Circles Are Not Concentric
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0112 (p. 78) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.5 <byrne-iii-5>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Intersecting blue and red circles with supposed common center, vertical yellow radius to upper intersection, and horizontal radius (solid black to red circle, dotted black to blue circle).
