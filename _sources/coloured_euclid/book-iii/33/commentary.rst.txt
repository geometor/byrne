Commentary on Proposition III.33: Segment Containing an Angle Equal to a Given Angle
====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0152 (p. 118) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.33 <byrne-iii-33>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue circle with horizontal red tangent at bottom, vertical blue+dotted-blue diameter from contact point, black chord, yellow radius, and red and yellow angle sectors.
