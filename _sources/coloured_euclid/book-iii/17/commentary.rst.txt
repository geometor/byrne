Commentary on Proposition III.17: Drawing a Tangent from an Exterior Point
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0132 (pp. 98–99) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.17 <byrne-iii-17>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Concentric red inner and yellow outer circles; dotted black + solid black horizontal radius, vertical blue tangent to inner circle, red+dotted-red radius to outer circle, dotted blue line from outer point, and blue and yellow angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.16`, :just:`I.4`.
