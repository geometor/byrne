Proposition III.2 — Chord Lies Wholly Within the Circle
=======================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition II. Theorem (*Pickering p. 75*)

A straight line (:byrne-line:`red solid`) joining two points in the circumference of a circle :byrne-circle:`red`, lies wholly within the circle.
