Proposition III.27 — Angles Standing on Equal Arcs Are Equal
============================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXVII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 111*)

In equal circles, :byrne-circle:`red` an :byrne-circle:`blue` the angles :byrne-angle:`yellow` and :byrne-angle:`red` which stand upon equal arches are equal, whether they be at the centres or at the circumferences.
