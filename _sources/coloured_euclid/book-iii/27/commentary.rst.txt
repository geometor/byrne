Commentary on Proposition III.27: Angles Standing on Equal Arcs Are Equal
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0145 (p. 111) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.27 <byrne-iii-27>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two equal circles (top red with black + dotted red arc, bottom blue with dotted black arc), each with inscribed and central angles marked in yellow, blue, and red sectors.
