Commentary on Proposition III.13: Circles Touch in at Most One Point
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0124 (pp. 90–91) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.13 <byrne-iii-13>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Three figures on 0124: Fig. I (yellow outer, blue inner circle touching internally with black, red, dotted blue lines); Fig. II (blue outer, black inner circle with vertical red diameter and two center ticks); Fig. III (yellow and blue circles touching externally with blue, black, red, and dotted red lines).
