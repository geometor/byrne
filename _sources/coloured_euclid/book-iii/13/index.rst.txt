Proposition III.13 — Circles Touch in at Most One Point
=======================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XIII. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 90–91*)

One circle cannot touch another, either externally or internally, in more points than one.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 124 (pp. 90–91)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Three figures on 0124: Fig. I (yellow outer, blue inner circle touching internally with black, red, dotted blue lines); Fig. II (blue outer, black inner circle with vertical red diameter and two center ticks); Fig. III (yellow and blue circles touching externally with blue, black, red, and dotted red lines).
