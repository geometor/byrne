Proposition III.10 — Two Circles Intersect in at Most Two Points
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition X. Theorem (*Pickering p. 86*)

One circle :byrne-circle:`blue` cannot intersect another :byrne-circle:`red` in more points than two.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 120 (p. 86)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Two diagrams of blue and red circles supposedly intersecting in three points (top: overlapping circles with black, blue, and yellow radii; bottom: indented red circle meeting blue circle in three points with yellow, black, and blue radii).
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** :just:`I.def.15`, :just:`III.5`, ``B. 3. prs. 7, 8``
