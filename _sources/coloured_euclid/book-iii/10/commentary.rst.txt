Commentary on Proposition III.10: Two Circles Intersect in at Most Two Points
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0120 (p. 86) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.10 <byrne-iii-10>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two diagrams of blue and red circles supposedly intersecting in three points (top: overlapping circles with black, blue, and yellow radii; bottom: indented red circle meeting blue circle in three points with yellow, black, and blue radii).
