Proposition III.12 — Line of Centres Passes Through External Contact
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 89*)

If two circles :byrne-circle:`blue` and :byrne-circle:`red` touch one another externally, the straight line (red-black-blue line) joining their centres, passes through the point of contact.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 123 (p. 89)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Blue and red circles touching externally, with horizontal red-black-blue line joining centers and dotted yellow and solid yellow radii to the point of contact.
