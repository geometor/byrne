Commentary on Proposition III.22: Opposite Angles of a Cyclic Quadrilateral
===========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0139 (p. 105) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.22 <byrne-iii-22>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Red circle with inscribed black quadrilateral, red and black diagonals, and split colored angle sectors at the four vertices (red/blue, yellow/red, yellow/black, black/blue).
