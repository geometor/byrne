Commentary on Proposition III.9: Point with Three Equal Segments Is the Centre
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0119 (p. 85) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.9 <byrne-iii-9>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Blue circle with interior point from which dotted yellow, solid yellow, and blue segments meet the circumference, and a red/black/dotted-red diameter through the point and supposed center.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.8`.
