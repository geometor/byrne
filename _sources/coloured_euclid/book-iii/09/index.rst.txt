Proposition III.9 — Point with Three Equal Segments Is the Centre
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition IX. Theorem (*Pickering p. 85*)

If a point be taken within a circle :byrne-circle:`blue`, from which more than two equal straight lines (:byrne-line:`yellow dotted`, :byrne-line:`yellow solid`, :byrne-line:`blue solid`) can be drawn to the circumference, that point must be the centre of the circle.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 119 (p. 85)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Blue circle with interior point from which dotted yellow, solid yellow, and blue segments meet the circumference, and a red/black/dotted-red diameter through the point and supposed center.
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** :just:`III.8`
