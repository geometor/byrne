Commentary on Proposition III.11: Line of Centres Passes Through Internal Contact
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0121 (pp. 87–88) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.11 <byrne-iii-11>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Black outer circle and blue inner circle touching at top; black line joining centers extended dotted yellow and solid blue, forming a yellow-filled triangle with red and dotted blue radii to the contact point.
