Book III Definitions (I–X)
==========================

.. rubric:: Equal Circles, Tangency, Chords & Circular Segments (*Pickering pp. [71]–73, Leaves 105–107*)

.. picture:: 0105.g1.png
   :alt: Book III Definitions polychrome woodblock diagrams: tangent circle, internal/external touching circles, and angle in a segment (Leaf 105, p. [71]).
   :caption: **Book III Definitions Archival Plate (Leaf 105, p. [71]).** Concentric/touching yellow, red, and blue circles alongside tangency and segment-angle figures.

.. picture:: 0105.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector plate for Book III Definitions.
   :caption: **Calibrated Vector Diagram (0105.g1).** Tangent circle, internally/externally touching circles, and inscribed angle in a segment.

* **Definition I:** *Equal circles* are those of which the diameters are equal, or from the centres of which the straight lines to the circumferences are equal.
* **Definitions II–III:** A straight line *touches* a circle when it meets it and being produced does not cut it; *circles touch one another* when they meet but do not cut.

.. folio:: 0106
   :page: 72

* **Definitions IV–VI:** Straight lines are *equally distant from the centre* when perpendiculars from the centre are equal; a *segment of a circle* is the figure contained by a straight line and an arc.

.. folio:: 0107
   :page: 73

* **Definitions VII–X:** *Angle of a segment*, *angle in a segment*, *standing upon a circumference*, *sector of a circle*, and *similar segments of circles* (containing equal angles).
