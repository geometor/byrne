Proposition III.24 — Similar Segments on Equal Chords Are Equal
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Book III, Proposition XXIV. Theorem (*Pickering p. 107*)

Similar segments (red segment with blue arc and black base) and (yellow segment with red arc and blue base), of circles upon equal straight lines (:byrne-line:`black solid` and :byrne-line:`blue solid`) are each equal to the other.
