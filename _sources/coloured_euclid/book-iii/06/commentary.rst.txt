Commentary on Proposition III.6: Internally Touching Circles Are Not Concentric
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0113 (p. 79) · Book III Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition III.6 <byrne-iii-6>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Red outer circle and black inner circle touching at top, yellow radius to contact point, and dotted+solid blue line from supposed common center cutting both circles.
