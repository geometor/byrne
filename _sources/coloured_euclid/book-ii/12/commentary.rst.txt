Commentary on Proposition II.12: Obtuse-Triangle Law of Cosines
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0101 (p. 67) · Book II Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition II.12 <byrne-ii-12>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Obtuse-angled triangle with a solid blue side opposite the obtuse angle, solid black base, and solid red right side; the black base is extended rightward as a black-dotted line to meet a vertical yellow perpendicular dropped from the apex.

**Logical Citations in Print:** ``pr. 4, B. 2``, ``pr. 47, B. 1``.
