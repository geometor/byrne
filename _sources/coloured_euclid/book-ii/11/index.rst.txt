Proposition II.11 — The Golden Section by Colored Areas
=======================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition XI. Problem (*Pickering p. 66* · ``G.154``)

*To divide a given straight line* (:byrne-line:`red solid-dotted <ii11:/ A H /; / H B />`) *into two parts, so that the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the parts may be equal to the square on the other part.*

.. picture:: /_static/figures/ii11.svg
   :alt: Book II Proposition 11 — Golden section construction showing blue golden square equal in area to yellow complement rectangle.
   :caption: **Book II, Prop. XI (p. 66 · G.154).** Pure :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)` area proof of the :term:`Golden Section <extreme-and-mean-ratio>` prior to Book V proportion theory.

.. picture:: 0100.g1.png
   :alt: Oliver Byrne 1847 Pickering archival full-RGB plate for Proposition II.11 (Leaf 100).
   :caption: **1847 Chiswick Press Archival Plate (Leaf 100, p. 66).** Full-RGB reproduction of Byrne's four-color woodblock print.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Construction.** Describe the unit square on the given line (:just:`I.46`), bisect the left vertical side at :math:`E` (:just:`I.10`), join oblique secant :byrne-line:`black solid <ii11:/ E B />` (:just:`I.post.1`), produce the vertical side upward to :math:`F` with :math:`EF = EB` (:just:`I.3`), and describe the golden square :byrne-sq:`blue filled <ii11:< A F G H >>` (:just:`I.46`), partitioning the main square into shared strip :byrne-rect:`blue outline <ii11:< A H K C >>` and right rectangle :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii11:< H B D K >>`.

   **Demonstration (:just:`II.6`, :just:`I.47`, :just:`C.N.3`).**

   By ``II.6`` applied to the bisected vertical side extended to :math:`F`, together with :just:`I.47` on the right triangle having hypotenuse :byrne-line:`black solid <ii11:/ E B />`:

   (:byrne-sq:`blue filled <ii11:< A F G H >>` + :byrne-rect:`blue outline <ii11:< A H K C >>`) = (:byrne-rect:`blue outline <ii11:< A H K C >>` + :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii11:< H B D K >>`).

   Subtracting the common strip :byrne-rect:`blue outline <ii11:< A H K C >>` from both (:just:`C.N.3`),

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-sq:`blue filled <ii11:< A F G H >>` = :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii11:< H B D K >>`, i.e. :byrne-line:`red solid squared <ii11:/ A H />` = whole line :byrne-op:`rect-by` :byrne-line:`red dotted <ii11:/ H B />`. **Q.E.F.**
