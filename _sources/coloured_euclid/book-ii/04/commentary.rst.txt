Commentary on Proposition II.4: Diagonal Half-Filled Squares & Visual Binomial Expansion
========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folios 0090–0091 (pp. 56–57) & Exact Q Area Identity (II.4 · G.147)

Dual-Role Diagonal Woodblocks
-----------------------------

In :func:`geometor.byrne.constructions.build_ii4`, dividing segment :math:`AB` of length :math:`5` into :math:`AC = 3` (:byrne-line:`blue solid <ii4:/ A C />`) and :math:`CB = 2` (:byrne-line:`red solid <ii4:/ C B />`) over :math:`\mathbb{Q}` partitions the :math:`25`-unit square into:

* Upper-left diagonal square :byrne-sq:`blue half <ii4:< D K G H >>` of area :math:`3^2 = 9`,
* Lower-right diagonal square :byrne-sq:`red half <ii4:< G F B C >>` of area :math:`2^2 = 4`, and
* Two congruent yellow complements :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii4:< H G C A >>`, :byrne-rect:`yellow filled <ii4:< K E F G >>` of area :math:`3 \times 2 = 6` each (:math:`9 + 4 + 2 \times 6 = 25`).

Byrne's half-filled square glyphs (:byrne-sq:`blue half`, :byrne-sq:`red half`) allow the diagonal triangles used in the ``I.6`` step and the completed squares used in the area sum to share a single unambiguous visual token.
