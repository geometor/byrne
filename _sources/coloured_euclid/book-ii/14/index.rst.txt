Proposition II.14 — Squaring Any Rectilineal Figure
===================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition XIV. Problem (*Pickering p. 70*)

TO draw a right line of which the square shall be equal to a given recti-linear figure. To draw :byrne-line:`blue solid` such that, :byrne-line:`blue solid squared` = :byrne-mag:`yellow pentagon`

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 104 (p. 70)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** Top: a solid yellow irregular pentagon. Left: a solid red rectangle whose top edge is extended leftward as a black-dotted line to form the diameter of a red semicircle, with a solid blue vertical perpendicular from the top-left corner of the rectangle to the semicircle, a solid red radius, and two black chords from that point on the semicircle to the endpoints of the diameter.
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** ``pr. 45, B. 1``, ``pr. 10, B. 1``, ``pr. 3, B. 1``, :just:`I.post.3`, ``pr. 5, B. 2``, ``pr. 47, B. 1``
