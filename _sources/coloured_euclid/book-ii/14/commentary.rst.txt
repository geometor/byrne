Commentary on Proposition II.14: Squaring Any Rectilineal Figure
================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0104 (p. 70) · Book II Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition II.14 <byrne-ii-14>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Top: a solid yellow irregular pentagon. Left: a solid red rectangle whose top edge is extended leftward as a black-dotted line to form the diameter of a red semicircle, with a solid blue vertical perpendicular from the top-left corner of the rectangle to the semicircle, a solid red radius, and two black chords from that point on the semicircle to the endpoints of the diameter.

**Logical Citations in Print:** ``pr. 45, B. 1``, ``pr. 10, B. 1``, ``pr. 3, B. 1``, :just:`I.post.3`, ``pr. 5, B. 2``, ``pr. 47, B. 1``.
