Proposition II.6 — Bisected Line Produced (Difference of Squares)
=================================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition VI. Theorem (*Pickering p. 60*)

IF a straight line be bisected (blue-yellow line) and produced to any point (red-blue-yellow line), the rectangle contained by the whole line so increased, and the part produced, together with the square of half the line, is equal to the square of the line made up of the half, and the produced part. (red-blue-yellow line) · :byrne-line:`red solid` + :byrne-line:`blue solid squared` = (red-blue line)².
