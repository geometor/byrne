Book II — Geometric Algebra & The Golden Section
================================================

.. rubric:: Coloured Rectangles, Gnomons & Extreme and Mean Ratio (*Pickering pp. 51–70, Leaves 85–104*)

.. folio:: 0085
   :page: 51

Book II translates quadratic algebraic identities into direct visual area decompositions—partitioning squares, contained rectangles (:byrne-op:`rect-by`), and :term:`gnomons <gnomon>` across the binomial square (:ref:`II.4 <byrne-ii-4>`), difference of squares (:ref:`II.5 <byrne-ii-5>`), and the Golden Section (:ref:`II.11 <byrne-ii-11>`).
