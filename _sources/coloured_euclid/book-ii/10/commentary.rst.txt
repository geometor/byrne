Commentary on Proposition II.10: Sum of Squares of Bisected and Produced Line
=============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0098 (pp. 64–65) · Book II Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition II.10 <byrne-ii-10>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Horizontal line divided into red, yellow, and blue segments, with a vertical black/red perpendicular at the midpoint, a blue-and-black-dotted slant line to the left vertex, a solid black line extending downward (black-dotted below the base) to meet a red-dotted perpendicular from the right end of the blue segment, a solid black chord to the left vertex, a yellow-dotted top horizontal line, and six colored angle sectors (black, yellow, blue, red).

**Logical Citations in Print:** ``pr. 31, B. 1``, ``pr. 5, B. 1``, ``cor. pr. 32, B. 1``, ``prs. 5, 32, 29, 34, B. 1``, ``prs. 6, 34, B. 1``, ``pr. 47, B. 1``.
