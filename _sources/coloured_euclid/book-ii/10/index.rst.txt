Proposition II.10 — Sum of Squares of Bisected and Produced Line
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition X. Theorem (*Pickering pp. 64–65*)

IF a straight line (red-yellow line) be bisected and produced to any point (red-yellow-blue line), the squares of the whole produced line, and of the produced part, are together double of the squares of the half line, and of the line made up of the half and produced part. (red-yellow-blue line)² + :byrne-line:`blue solid squared` = 2 :byrne-line:`yellow solid squared` + 2 (yellow-blue line)².
