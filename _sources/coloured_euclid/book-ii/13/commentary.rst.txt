Commentary on Proposition II.13: Acute-Triangle Law of Cosines
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0102 (pp. 68–69) · Book II Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition II.13 <byrne-ii-13>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Two triangles side-by-side labeled FIRST. and SECOND. FIRST has a red left side, blue right side, solid yellow interior vertical altitude, and a base split into solid black (left) and black-dotted (right). SECOND has a red left side, blue right side, solid black base extended rightward as a black-dotted segment to meet a vertical yellow-dotted altitude.

**Logical Citations in Print:** ``pr. 7, B. 2``, ``pr. 47, B. 1``.
