Proposition II.8 — Four Times Rectangle Plus Square on Remainder
================================================================

.. rubric:: Book II, Proposition VIII. Theorem (*Pickering p. 62*)

IF a straight line be divided into any two parts (blue-red line), the square of the sum of the whole line and any one of its parts, is equal to four times the rectangle contained by the whole line, and that part together with the square of the other part. (blue-red-yellow line)² = 4 · (blue-red line) · :byrne-line:`red solid` + :byrne-line:`blue solid squared`,
