Commentary on Proposition II.9: Sum of Squares of Equal and Unequal Segments
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0097 (p. 63) · Book II Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition II.9 <byrne-ii-9>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` theorem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Right isosceles triangle with a horizontal base divided into blue, yellow, and red segments, black-dotted left leg, black-and-yellow-dotted right leg, vertical blue midline (dotted upper, solid lower), vertical red-dotted perpendicular, horizontal and slant solid black lines, and six colored angle sectors (yellow, red, black, blue).

**Logical Citations in Print:** ``pr. 5, B. 1``, ``cor. pr. 32, B. 1``, ``prs. 5, 29, B. 1``, ``prs. 6, 34, B. 1``, ``pr. 47, B. 1``.
