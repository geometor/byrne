Commentary on Book II Definitions
=================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Leaves 85–86 (pp. 51–52)

Book II introduces Byrne's solid-fill rectangular algebra notation (:byrne-op:`rect-by` and ``²``) and the L-shaped :term:`gnomon <gnomon>`, setting the visual grammar for ``II.1``–``II.14``.
