Book II Definitions (I–II)
==========================

.. rubric:: Contained Rectangles & The Gnomon (*Pickering pp. 51–52, Leaves 85–86*)

.. picture:: 0085.g1.png
   :alt: Oliver Byrne 1847 Book II Definition I solid Prussian-blue rectangle bounded by red and black sides (Leaf 85, p. 51).
   :caption: **Book II, Definition I Archival Plate (Leaf 85, p. 51).** Contained right-angled parallelogram bounded by :byrne-line:`red solid` and :byrne-line:`black solid`.

.. picture:: 0085.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated 5-layer vector diagram of Book II Definition I solid blue rectangle.
   :caption: **Calibrated Vector Diagram (0085.g1).** Theme-adaptive 5-layer vector plate for Book II Definition I.

* **Definition I (p. 51):** Every right-angled parallelogram (:byrne-rect:`blue filled`) is said to be *contained* (:byrne-op:`rect-by`) by any two of the straight lines (:byrne-line:`black solid`, :byrne-line:`red solid`) which contain one of the right angles.

.. folio:: 0086
   :page: 52

* **Definition II (p. 52):** In every parallelogram, any of the parallelograms about a diameter, together with the two complements, is called a :term:`gnomon <gnomon>`.
