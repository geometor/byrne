Byrne–Simson–Heath–G-Index Concordance
======================================

.. rubric:: Cross-Repository Alignment Across ``geometor/byrne``, ``geometor/euclid``, ``geometor/elements`` & ``geometor/codes``

Because :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` adopted :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` :cite:`1756 Glasgow numbering <simson:1756>`, several Book I definitions and axioms carry numbers distinct from the critical Heiberg/:person:`Heath <heath>` text (:cite:`1908 <heath:1908>`, ``geometor/euclid``) and the ``geometor/elements`` G-Index.
