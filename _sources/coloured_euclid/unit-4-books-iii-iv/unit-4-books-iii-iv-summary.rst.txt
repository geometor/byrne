Unit IV Summary: From Circle Invariants to the Regular Pentagon
===============================================================

.. rubric:: How Books I, II & III Converge in Book IV, Propositions 10–11

Books III and IV weave together every preceding thread of the *Elements*:

1. **Inscribed & Semicircular Angles (``III.20``, ``III.31``)**:
   Isosceles radius triangles (``I.5``) and the exterior-angle theorem (``I.32``) establish that central angles double inscribed angles and that every angle inscribed in a semicircle is right.
2. **Power of a Point (``III.35``–``III.37``)**:
   Book II geometric algebra (``II.5``–``II.6``) combined with the Pythagorean theorem (``I.47``) proves that the square on a tangent equals the rectangle of any secant and its external part.
3. **The Pentagonal Crown (``IV.10``–``IV.11``)**:
   Cutting a radius in golden ratio via ``II.11`` and recognizing tangent contact via ``III.37`` constructs the $36^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ$ Golden Triangle (``IV.10``), whose angle bisectors immediately inscribe the regular pentagon (``IV.11``).
