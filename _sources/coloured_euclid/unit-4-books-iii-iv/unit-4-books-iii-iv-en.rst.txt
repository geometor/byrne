Book III, Propositions XX & XXXI (*Inscribed Angles & Thales's Theorem*, pp. 102, 115)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iii20.svg
   :alt: Book III Proposition 20 — Blue circle with central blue angle double the inscribed yellow angle.
   :caption: **Book III, Prop. XX (p. 102 · G.185).** The angle at the centre :byrne-angle:`blue <iii20:< B O C )>` is double the angle at the circumference :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>`.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Prop. XX Demonstration (:just:`I.5`, :just:`I.32`).** Drawing diameter :byrne-line:`red solid <iii20:/ A O />` + :byrne-line:`red dotted <iii20:/ O D />`, isosceles radii make base angles :byrne-angle:`red <iii20:< O B A )>` equal to the corresponding part of :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>`, so each exterior central sector doubles its inscribed half;

   :byrne-op:`therefore` :byrne-angle:`blue <iii20:< B O C )>` = 2 × :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii20:< B A C )>`. **Q.E.D.**

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iii31.svg
   :alt: Book III Proposition 31 — Thales's Theorem showing a right angle inscribed in a semicircle split by a red median radius.
   :caption: **Book III, Prop. XXXI (p. 115 · G.196).** Thales's Theorem: the angle in a semicircle (:byrne-angle:`yellow <iii31:< B A O )>` + :byrne-angle:`black <iii31:< O A C )>`) is a right angle (:byrne-op:`right-angle`).

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Prop. XXXI Demonstration (:just:`I.5`, :just:`I.32`).** Since radii :byrne-line:`blue solid <iii31:/ B O />` = :byrne-line:`red solid <iii31:/ O A />` = :byrne-line:`black solid <iii31:/ O C />`, isosceles triangles give :byrne-angle:`yellow <iii31:< B A O )>` = :byrne-angle:`red <iii31:< O B A )>` and :byrne-angle:`black <iii31:< O A C )>` = :byrne-angle:`blue <iii31:< A C O )>`. By ``I.32``, (:byrne-angle:`yellow <iii31:< B A O )>` + :byrne-angle:`black <iii31:< O A C )>`) equals half of :byrne-op:`two-right-angles` = :byrne-op:`right-angle`. **Q.E.D.**

Book III, Proposition XXXVI (*Tangent-Secant Power of a Point*, p. 121 · ``G.201``)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iii36.svg
   :alt: Book III Proposition 36 — External blue tangent and red-black secant through the center of a black circle.
   :caption: **Book III, Prop. XXXVI (p. 121).** Square on tangent :byrne-line:`blue solid <iii36:/ D T />` equals the rectangle contained by whole secant and external part :byrne-line:`red solid <iii36:/ D C />`.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Demonstration (:just:`III.18`, :just:`II.6`, :just:`I.47`).** Because contact radius :byrne-line:`yellow solid <iii36:/ O T />` :byrne-op:`perp` tangent :byrne-line:`blue solid <iii36:/ D T />` (:byrne-angle:`yellow <iii36:< D T O )>` = :byrne-op:`right-angle`), applying ``II.6`` to the bisected diameter (:byrne-line:`red dotted <iii36:/ C O />` + :byrne-line:`black solid <iii36:/ O A />`) plus external :byrne-line:`red solid <iii36:/ D C />` yields:

   (whole secant :byrne-op:`rect-by` :byrne-line:`red solid <iii36:/ D C />`) + :byrne-line:`red dotted squared <iii36:/ C O />` = (:byrne-line:`red solid <iii36:/ D C />` + :byrne-line:`red dotted <iii36:/ C O />`)² = :byrne-line:`yellow solid squared <iii36:/ O T />` + :byrne-line:`blue solid squared <iii36:/ D T />`.

   Canceling equal radius squares (:byrne-line:`red dotted <iii36:/ C O />` = :byrne-line:`yellow solid <iii36:/ O T />`), :byrne-op:`therefore` whole secant :byrne-op:`rect-by` :byrne-line:`red solid <iii36:/ D C />` = :byrne-line:`blue solid squared <iii36:/ D T />`. **Q.E.D.**

Book IV, Propositions IV, X & XI (*Incircle, Golden Triangle & Regular Pentagon*, pp. 129–137)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iv4.svg
   :alt: Book IV Proposition 4 — Incircle of a triangle constructed from two blue angle bisectors and three dotted perpendicular inradii.
   :caption: **Book IV, Prop. IV (p. 129 · G.217).** Inscribing :byrne-circle:`black <iv4:( I E )>` inside a given triangle (:byrne-line:`yellow solid <iv4:/ A B />`, :byrne-line:`red solid <iv4:/ A C />`, :byrne-line:`black solid <iv4:/ B C />`).

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iv10.svg
   :alt: Book IV Proposition 10 — Construction of the 36-72-72 degree Golden Triangle via red and blue circles.
   :caption: **Book IV, Prop. X (pp. 135–136 · G.223).** The $36^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ$ Golden Triangle uniting ``II.11`` and ``III.37``.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Prop. X Synthesis (:just:`II.11`, :just:`IV.5`, :just:`III.37`, :just:`III.32`).** Cut radius :byrne-line:`black solid <iv10:/ A C />` + :byrne-line:`black dotted <iv10:/ C B />` in golden ratio (:just:`II.11`), fit base chord :byrne-line:`blue solid <iv10:/ B D />` = :byrne-line:`black solid <iv10:/ A C />` inside :byrne-circle:`red <iv10:( A B )>`, and circumscribe :byrne-circle:`blue <iv10:( P A )>`. Since :byrne-line:`blue solid squared <iv10:/ B D />` equals whole radius :byrne-op:`rect-by` :byrne-line:`black dotted <iv10:/ C B />`, :byrne-line:`blue solid <iv10:/ B D />` is tangent to :byrne-circle:`blue <iv10:( P A )>` (:just:`III.37`), whence :byrne-angle:`yellow <iv10:< C D B )>` = apex :byrne-angle:`red <iv10:< B A D )>` (:just:`III.32`) and each base angle :byrne-angle:`blue <iv10:< D B A )>` = 2 × :byrne-angle:`red <iv10:< B A D )>`. **Q.E.F.**

.. picture:: /_static/figures/iv11.svg
   :alt: Book IV Proposition 11 — Regular pentagon inscribed in a blue circle around a solid yellow Golden Triangle.
   :caption: **Book IV, Prop. XI (p. 137 · G.224).** Regular pentagon inscribed in :byrne-circle:`blue <iv11:( O A )>` by bisecting the base angles of inscribed Golden Triangle :byrne-tri:`yellow filled <iv11:< A C D >>`.

.. container:: byrne-eq-block

   **Prop. XI Demonstration (:just:`IV.10`, :just:`IV.2`, :just:`I.9`, :just:`III.26`, :just:`III.29`).**

   Bisecting both $72^\circ$ base angles of :byrne-tri:`yellow filled <iv11:< A C D >>` by star diagonals :byrne-line:`black solid <iv11:/ C E /; / D B />` produces five equal $36^\circ$ inscribed angles (:byrne-angle:`black <iv11:< C A D )>`, :byrne-angle:`blue <iv11:< E C A )>`, :byrne-angle:`black <iv11:< D C E )>`, :byrne-angle:`red <iv11:< B D C )>`), subtending five equal arcs and five equal pentagon chords: :byrne-line:`red solid <iv11:/ A B />` = :byrne-line:`red dotted <iv11:/ B C />` = :byrne-line:`black solid <iv11:/ C D />` = :byrne-line:`yellow solid <iv11:/ D E />` = :byrne-line:`blue solid <iv11:/ E A />`. **Q.E.F.**
