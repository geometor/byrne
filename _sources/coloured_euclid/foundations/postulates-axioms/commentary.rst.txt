Commentary on Postulates & Axioms
=================================

.. rubric:: Leaves 26–27 (pp. xxii–xxiii) · Simson Axioms XI–XII

Following :person:`Robert Simson <simson>`, Euclid's Fourth and Fifth Postulates appear here as **Axiom XI** and **Axiom XII**. Byrne illustrates Axiom XII visually on p. xxiii using red and blue cut straight lines with yellow and red interior wedges summed against :byrne-op:`two-right-angles`.
