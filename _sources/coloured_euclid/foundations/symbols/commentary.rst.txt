Commentary on Byrne's Operator Symbols
======================================

.. rubric:: Leaves 31–32 (pp. xxvii–xxviii)

Byrne's operator repertoire deliberately repurposes typographic marks (notably rotated/open square brackets ``⊏`` and ``⊐`` for *greater than* and *less than*, and quadrant/semicircle glyphs for right angles) so spatial proofs read as clean algebraic equations.
