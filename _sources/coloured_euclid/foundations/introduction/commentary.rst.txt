Commentary on Byrne's 1847 Introduction
=======================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Leaves 11–21 (pp. vii–xvii)

Byrne's *Introduction* presents the theoretical justification for :term:`pointer-free visual notation <pointer-free-notation>`: eliminating the mental pointer table between text and figure lowers working-memory load across every Euclidean step. Leaves ``0013``–``0021`` progress smoothly from a single right-angled triangle to full comparative benchmarks on the *Pons Asinorum* (:ref:`I.5 <byrne-i-5>`).
