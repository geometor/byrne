Commentary on Proposition IV.13: Inscribing a Circle in a Regular Pentagon
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0174 (pp. 140–141) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.13 <byrne-iv-13>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A pentagon with black, black-dotted, and yellow sides enclosing an inscribed yellow circle; radial lines (black, red, dotted blue, dotted yellow, dotted red, vertical blue) connect the center to vertices and side midpoints, with yellow, blue, black, and red angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.9`, :just:`I.4`, *const*, :just:`I.26`, :just:`III.16`.
