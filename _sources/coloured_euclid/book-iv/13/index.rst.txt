Proposition IV.13 — Inscribing a Circle in a Regular Pentagon
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Book IV, Proposition XIII. Problem (*Pickering pp. 140–141*)

To inscribe a circle in a given equiangular and equilateral pentagon.
