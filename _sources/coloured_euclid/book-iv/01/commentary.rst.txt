Commentary on Proposition IV.1: Placing a Chord Equal to a Given Line
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0159 (p. 125) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.1 <byrne-iv-1>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A yellow given circle with a horizontal red dotted/solid diameter, intersected by a larger blue circle centered at the left extremity of the diameter, a yellow chord to the upper intersection, and a short blue reference line below.

**Logical Citations in Print:** *hyp*, :just:`I.3`, :just:`I.def.15`, *const*.
