Proposition IV.15 — Regular Hexagon Inscribed in a Circle
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Book IV, Proposition XV. Problem (*Pickering p. 143*)

To inscribe an equilateral and equiangular hexagon in a given circle :byrne-circle:`yellow`.

Pickering 1847 Visual & Deductive Record
----------------------------------------

* **Archival Folio Witness:** Leaf 177 (p. 143)
* **Chiswick Press Visual Design:** A yellow given circle intersected by a lower red circle passing through its center; three diameters (black vertical, blue, yellow) define an inscribed hexagon (solid black upper sides, dotted black, dotted red, dotted blue lower/right sides) with red, blue, and black central angle sectors.
* **Cited Deductive Dependencies:** :just:`I.32`, :just:`I.13`, :just:`I.15`, :just:`III.26`, :just:`III.29`
