Commentary on Proposition IV.6: Inscribing a Square in a Circle
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0165 (p. 131) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.6 <byrne-iv-6>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A red circle with two perpendicular diameters (horizontal red/blue dotted, vertical black dotted/solid) and an inscribed square with black, red, blue, and yellow sides, plus blue and red central right-angle sectors and yellow and black inscribed right-angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.31`, :just:`I.28`, *const*, :just:`I.def.15`, :just:`I.4`, :just:`I.34`.
