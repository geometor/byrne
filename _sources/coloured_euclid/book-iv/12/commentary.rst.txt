Commentary on Proposition IV.12: Regular Pentagon Circumscribed About a Circle
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0172 (pp. 138–139) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.12 <byrne-iv-12>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. A red circle circumscribed by a black-outlined pentagon (with dotted blue left side and red/yellow bottom side); from the center, dotted red, dotted yellow, dotted black, solid blue, and solid black lines radiate to vertices and tangent points, with red, blue, black, and yellow angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`III.17`, :just:`I.47`, :just:`I.8`, :just:`III.27`.
