Commentary on Proposition IV.3: Circumscribing a Triangle Equiangular to a Given Triangle
=========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Pickering Folio 0161 (pp. 127–128) · Book IV Chromatic Apparatus

Pointer-Free Proof Structure
----------------------------

In :ref:`Proposition IV.3 <byrne-iv-3>`, :person:`Oliver Byrne <byrne>` renders :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` problem from :person:`Robert Simson's <simson>` text into self-naming colour and symbol registers without alphabetic vertex labels. Upper left shows the given triangle on an extended black base with yellow/red, blue/outlined, and black/red angle sectors; lower right shows a red circle circumscribed by a large triangle (blue base, yellow left side, dotted red right side) with three radii (red, yellow, black) and yellow, blue, black, and red angle sectors.

**Logical Citations in Print:** :just:`I.23`, :just:`III.17`, :just:`I.32`, *const*, :just:`I.13`.
