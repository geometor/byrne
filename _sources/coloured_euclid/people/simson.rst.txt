Simson
======

**Robert Simson** (1687 – 1768) — *Scottish Geometer & Professor at Glasgow*
   Editor of the influential 1756 *Elements of Euclid*, whose numbering of 35 Definitions, 3 Postulates, and 12 Axioms governs Byrne's marginal citations throughout Books I–VI.
