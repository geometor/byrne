Modules¶

geometor.byrne¶

Digital Scriptorium Codex, Visual Pseudocode Engine, and Symbolic Proofs for Oliver Byrne’s 1847 Euclid.

Unites Oliver Byrne’s The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid in Which Coloured Diagrams and Symbols Are Used Instead of Letters for the Greater Ease of Learners (London: William Pickering / Chiswick Press, 1847) with:

  1. Inline Byrne Visual Glyph Roles (geometor.byrne.sphinx_ext) rendering authentic four-color vector micro-glyphs inside Sphinx/HTML/EPUB proof equations with bidirectional .euclid-graphic hover/click highlighting;

  2. Formal Pointer-Free Visual Pseudocode Grammar (geometor.byrne.pseudocode) modeling Byrne’s 1847 notation as a typed geometric value-token DSL alongside Heath’s classical alphabetic Euclid and geometor/elements formal pseudocode; and

  3. 100% Exact Symbolic Constructions & Renderer (geometor.byrne.constructions, geometor.byrne.render) built on geometor.model.Model over exact SymPy number fields ($mathbb{Q}$, $mathbb{Q}(sqrt{3})$, $mathbb{Q}(phi)$, and cyclotomic pentagonal extensions).
