geometor.byrne.constructions¶

Exact symbolic geometor.model constructions for 17 Oliver Byrne (1847) showcase plates.

Every construction executes with geometor.model.Model (auto_intersect=False, zero float or sp.Float coordinates) over exact SymPy algebraic fields:

  • $\mathbb{Q}$: I.4, I.5, I.15, I.32, I.46, I.47, II.4, II.5, III.20, III.31, III.36, IV.4

  • $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$: I.1

  • $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$ where $\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$: II.11, VI.30

  • $\mathbb{Q}(\phi, \sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}})$: IV.10, IV.11

Each ByrnePlate pairs the populated symbolic Model and verified algebraic proof identities with authentic 1847 Chiswick Press four-color visual layers calibrated against the William Pickering scan (Internet Archive firstsixbooksofe00eucl).
